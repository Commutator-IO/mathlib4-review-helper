New file on the fixed points of an adjunction $L \dashv R$ ($L : C \to D$, unit $\eta$, counit $\varepsilon$): the full subcategories $\mathrm{Fix}(\eta) \subseteq C$ and $\mathrm{Fix}(\varepsilon) \subseteq D$ where the unit, resp. counit, is invertible, and the equivalence $\mathrm{Fix}(\eta) \simeq \mathrm{Fix}(\varepsilon)$ induced by $L, R$ (valid for every adjunction). It proves the four standard characterizations of an *idempotent* adjunction ($\eta R$, $R\varepsilon$, $\varepsilon L$, $L\eta$ invertible) are equivalent, and that for an idempotent adjunction $\mathrm{Fix}(\eta)$ is reflective with reflector $RL$ and $\mathrm{Fix}(\varepsilon)$ coreflective with coreflector $LR$ (Borceux, Handbook 1, Prop. 3.4.1). Built on `ObjectProperty`, `Reflective`/`Coreflective`.

## isIso_iff_of_comp_eq_id

(Private.) If $f : X \to Y$, $g : Y \to X$ with $g \circ f = \mathrm{id}_X$, then $f$ is an isomorphism iff $g$ is.

## idempotent_tfae

For an adjunction $L \dashv R$, the following are equivalent: (1) $\eta_{RY}$ is an iso for all $Y \in D$; (2) $R\varepsilon_Y$ is an iso for all $Y$; (3) $\varepsilon_{LX}$ is an iso for all $X \in C$; (4) $L\eta_X$ is an iso for all $X$.

**Proof.** (1)⇔(2) and (3)⇔(4) from the triangle identities $R\varepsilon_Y \circ \eta_{RY} = \mathrm{id}$, $\varepsilon_{LX} \circ L\eta_X = \mathrm{id}$ and the private lemma. (2)⇒(3): show $RL\eta_X = \eta_{RLX}$ (both are sections of the iso $R\varepsilon_{LX}$), then $L\eta_X$ is a two-sided inverse of $\varepsilon_{LX}$ using naturality of $\varepsilon$. (4)⇒(1) dually, via $LR\varepsilon_Y = \varepsilon_{LRY}$.

## map_map_unit_app_of_idempotent

If $\eta_{RY}$ is an iso for all $Y$ (condition (1)), then $RL\eta_X = \eta_{RLX}$ for all $X \in C$.

**Proof.** By (1)⇒(2) $R\varepsilon_{LX}$ is an iso; cancel it on the right, both composites are the identity by the triangle identity and naturality.

**Check.** The same argument is repeated verbatim as `key` inside the (2)⇒(3) step of `idempotent_tfae`; one could be derived from the other.

## map_map_counit_app_of_idempotent

If $\varepsilon_{LX}$ is an iso for all $X$ (condition (3)), then $LR\varepsilon_Y = \varepsilon_{LRY}$ for all $Y \in D$.

**Proof.** By (3)⇒(4) $L\eta_{RY}$ is an iso; cancel it on the left. Dual of the previous lemma (also duplicated inside `idempotent_tfae`).

## fixedLeft

**Definition.** The object property on $C$: $X$ satisfies it iff $\eta_X : X \to RLX$ is an isomorphism.

## fixedRight

**Definition.** The object property on $D$: $Y$ satisfies it iff $\varepsilon_Y : LRY \to Y$ is an isomorphism.

## fixedLeft_iff

$X \in \mathrm{Fix}(\eta)$ iff $\eta_X$ is an iso.

**Proof.** `Iff.rfl`.

## fixedRight_iff

$Y \in \mathrm{Fix}(\varepsilon)$ iff $\varepsilon_Y$ is an iso.

**Proof.** `Iff.rfl`.

## fixedRight_obj

If $\eta_X$ is an iso then $\varepsilon_{LX}$ is an iso, i.e. $L$ maps $\mathrm{Fix}(\eta)$ into $\mathrm{Fix}(\varepsilon)$.

**Proof.** Triangle identity $\varepsilon_{LX} \circ L\eta_X = \mathrm{id}$ with $L\eta_X$ an iso.

## fixedLeft_obj

If $\varepsilon_Y$ is an iso then $\eta_{RY}$ is an iso, i.e. $R$ maps $\mathrm{Fix}(\varepsilon)$ into $\mathrm{Fix}(\eta)$.

**Proof.** Triangle identity $R\varepsilon_Y \circ \eta_{RY} = \mathrm{id}$ with $R\varepsilon_Y$ an iso.

## fixedLeftUnitIso

**Definition.** For $X \in \mathrm{Fix}(\eta)$, the isomorphism $X \cong RLX$ given by $\eta_X$.

## fixedLeftUnitIso_hom

The forward map of `fixedLeftUnitIso X` is $\eta_X$ (simp lemma, `rfl`).

## fixedRightCounitIso

**Definition.** For $Y \in \mathrm{Fix}(\varepsilon)$, the isomorphism $LRY \cong Y$ given by $\varepsilon_Y$.

## fixedRightCounitIso_hom

The forward map of `fixedRightCounitIso Y` is $\varepsilon_Y$ (simp lemma, `rfl`).

## fixedRight_iff_exists

$Y \in \mathrm{Fix}(\varepsilon)$ iff $Y \cong LX$ for some $X \in \mathrm{Fix}(\eta)$; i.e. $\mathrm{Fix}(\varepsilon)$ is the essential image of $L$ restricted to $\mathrm{Fix}(\eta)$.

**Proof.** ($\Rightarrow$) take $X = RY$ and the iso $\varepsilon_Y$. ($\Leftarrow$) `fixedRight_obj` and closure of $\mathrm{Fix}(\varepsilon)$ under isomorphisms.

## fixedLeft_iff_exists

$X \in \mathrm{Fix}(\eta)$ iff $X \cong RY$ for some $Y \in \mathrm{Fix}(\varepsilon)$.

**Proof.** Dual of the previous one, with $Y = LX$ and the iso $\eta_X^{-1}$.

## fixedLeftToFixedRight

**Definition.** The restriction of $L$ to a functor $\mathrm{Fix}(\eta) \to \mathrm{Fix}(\varepsilon)$ (well defined by `fixedRight_obj`).

## fixedRightToFixedLeft

**Definition.** The restriction of $R$ to a functor $\mathrm{Fix}(\varepsilon) \to \mathrm{Fix}(\eta)$ (well defined by `fixedLeft_obj`).

## fixedEquiv

For any adjunction $L \dashv R$, the restrictions of $L$ and $R$ form an equivalence of categories $\mathrm{Fix}(\eta) \simeq \mathrm{Fix}(\varepsilon)$.

**Definition.** Functor `fixedLeftToFixedRight`, inverse `fixedRightToFixedLeft`, unit and counit isomorphisms given componentwise by $\eta_X$ and $\varepsilon_Y$ (naturality from that of $\eta$, $\varepsilon$); the triangle law is the left triangle identity of the adjunction.

**Check.** `@[simps]` on a full `Equivalence` structure generates simp lemmas for every field (including `unitIso`, `counitIso`); usually `simps` is restricted to the needed projections.

## fixedLeftReflector

**Definition.** Given $h$: $\varepsilon_{LX}$ is an iso for all $X$ (condition (3) of `idempotent_tfae`), the functor $C \to \mathrm{Fix}(\eta)$, $X \mapsto RLX$ (lands in $\mathrm{Fix}(\eta)$ by `fixedLeft_obj`).

**Check.** Idempotency is passed as a raw hypothesis `h : ∀ X, adj.fixedRight (L.obj X)` (and a different one of the four forms for the coreflective side); a named predicate/class `Adjunction.IsIdempotent` might be cleaner.

## fixedLeftAdjunction

Given $h$ as above, the functor $X \mapsto RLX$, $C \to \mathrm{Fix}(\eta)$, is left adjoint to the inclusion $\mathrm{Fix}(\eta) \hookrightarrow C$.

**Definition.** Unit $\eta_X : X \to RLX$; counit at $X \in \mathrm{Fix}(\eta)$ is $\eta_X^{-1}$. The right triangle is $\eta_X^{-1}\eta_X = \mathrm{id}$; the left triangle uses $RL\eta_X = \eta_{RLX}$ (`map_map_unit_app_of_idempotent`, with condition (1) obtained from (3) via `idempotent_tfae`).

## fixedLeftReflective

Given $h$ as above, the inclusion $\mathrm{Fix}(\eta) \hookrightarrow C$ is reflective, with reflector $RL$.

**Definition.** Bundles `fixedLeftReflector` and `fixedLeftAdjunction` (an `abbrev`, not an instance, since it depends on $h$).

## fixedRightCoreflector

**Definition.** Given $h$: $\eta_{RY}$ is an iso for all $Y$ (condition (1)), the functor $D \to \mathrm{Fix}(\varepsilon)$, $Y \mapsto LRY$.

## fixedRightAdjunction

Given $h$ as above, the inclusion $\mathrm{Fix}(\varepsilon) \hookrightarrow D$ is left adjoint to $Y \mapsto LRY$.

**Definition.** Unit at $Y \in \mathrm{Fix}(\varepsilon)$ is $\varepsilon_Y^{-1}$; counit $\varepsilon_Y$. Left triangle is $\varepsilon_Y \varepsilon_Y^{-1} = \mathrm{id}$; right triangle uses $LR\varepsilon_Y = \varepsilon_{LRY}$ (`map_map_counit_app_of_idempotent`, condition (3) from (1)).

## fixedRightCoreflective

Given $h$ as above, the inclusion $\mathrm{Fix}(\varepsilon) \hookrightarrow D$ is coreflective, with coreflector $LR$.

**Definition.** Bundles `fixedRightCoreflector` and `fixedRightAdjunction`.
