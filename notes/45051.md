Two identities in $\operatorname{End}_R(A)$, for $A$ a commutative $R$-algebra, relating an $R$-derivation $D : A \to A$ with the multiplication operators $L_a : b \mapsto ab$. The first is the operator form of the Leibniz rule; the second computes the conjugate of $D$ by multiplication by a unit, which differs from $D$ by multiplication by the logarithmic derivative. Builds on the existing Lie structure on `Derivation R A A` and `Ring.lie_def` on `Module.End`.

## lie_mulLeft

For a commutative ring $R$, a commutative $R$-algebra $A$, $a \in A$ and an $R$-derivation $D : A \to A$, the commutator in $\operatorname{End}_R(A)$ satisfies
$$[D, L_a] = D \circ L_a - L_a \circ D = L_{D(a)},$$
i.e. $D(ab) - aD(b) = D(a)\,b$ for all $b$.

**Proof.** Extensionality, then `simp` with the definition of the ring commutator, the Leibniz rule and commutativity.

## mulLeft_mul_mul_mulLeft_inv

For $R$, $A$ as above, an $R$-derivation $D : A \to A$ and a unit $u \in A^\times$,
$$L_u \circ D \circ L_{u^{-1}} = D - L_{u^{-1} D(u)}$$
in $\operatorname{End}_R(A)$; that is, $u\,D(u^{-1}b) = D(b) - u^{-1}D(u)\,b$.

**Proof.** Extensionality at $b$, then `simp` with the Leibniz rule and `leibniz_of_mul_eq_one` (giving $D(u^{-1}) = -u^{-2}D(u)$), followed by ring normalisation via `mul_assoc`, `mul_comm`, `sq`.
