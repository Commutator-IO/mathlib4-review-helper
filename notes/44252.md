Proves the determinant formula for the volume of a simplex: in a Euclidean affine space of dimension $n$, an $n$-simplex has volume $\frac{1}{n!}\,|\omega(P_1 - P_0, \dots, P_n - P_0)|$, with $\omega$ the volume form of an orientation (`Orientation.volumeForm`). The route: Hausdorff measure scales by `normDet` under affine maps (extending the existing linear-map result in `NormDet.lean`), the corner simplex of #44462 has volume $1/n!$, and every simplex is the image of the corner simplex under its `cornerMap`.

## hausdorffMeasure_image

For finite-dimensional real inner product spaces $U, V$, metric affine spaces $P$ over $U$ and $Q$ over $V$ (Borel), an affine map $f : P \to Q$ and any set $S \subseteq P$, with $d = \dim U$:
$$\mu_H^d(f(S)) = \operatorname{normDet}(f_{\text{lin}}) \cdot \mu_H^d(S),$$
where $\mu_H^d$ is the $d$-dimensional Hausdorff measure and $f_{\text{lin}}$ the linear part of $f$. No injectivity is assumed (normDet is $0$ for non-injective maps).

**Proof.** Write $f$ as (translate) $\circ f_{\text{lin}} \circ$ (untranslate) around an arbitrary base point; the translations are isometries, so they preserve $\mu_H^d$, and the linear case is `LinearMap.hausdorffMeasure_image`.

## euclideanHausdorffMeasure_image

The same scaling law for the normalised Euclidean Hausdorff measure $\mu_{HE}^d$ (a constant multiple of $\mu_H^d$): $\mu_{HE}^d(f(S)) = \operatorname{normDet}(f_{\text{lin}}) \cdot \mu_{HE}^d(S)$.

**Proof.** Unfold $\mu_{HE}$ as a scalar multiple of $\mu_H$, apply the previous lemma, commute the factors.

## volume_map_of_finrank_eq

If the direction $V$ of $P$ has dimension $n$ (`hn : finrank ℝ V = n`), $s$ is an $n$-simplex in $P$ and $f : P \to P_2$ is an injective affine map, then
$$\operatorname{vol}(f(s)) = \operatorname{normDet}(f_{\text{lin}}) \cdot \operatorname{vol}(s).$$

**Proof.** Express volume as the Euclidean Hausdorff measure of the closed interior, use that the closed interior commutes with `map`, and apply `euclideanHausdorffMeasure_image`; `normDet_nonneg` handles the `ofReal`/`toReal` conversion.

## volume_cornerSimplex

The $n$-dimensional corner simplex (origin and the $n$ standard unit vectors in $\mathbb{R}^n$) has volume $1/n!$. Simp lemma.

**Proof.** Induction on $n$: apply `volume_eq` at vertex $1$ (height $1$ by `height_cornerSimplex`), the opposite face is an isometric copy of the $n$-dimensional corner simplex (`faceOpposite_cornerSimplex`, `volume_map`), so $\operatorname{vol}_{n+1} = \frac{1}{n+1}\cdot 1 \cdot \frac{1}{n!}$.

## volume_eq_volumeForm

If $V$ has dimension $n$ and $o$ is an orientation of $V$, every $n$-simplex $s$ in $P$ satisfies
$$\operatorname{vol}(s) = \frac{1}{n!}\,\bigl|\omega_o(P_1 - P_0, \dots, P_n - P_0)\bigr|.$$

**Proof.** $s$ is the image of the corner simplex under `s.cornerMap` (`map_cornerSimplex_cornerMap`); apply `volume_map_of_finrank_eq`, `volume_cornerSimplex` and `normDet_cornerMap_linear`.
