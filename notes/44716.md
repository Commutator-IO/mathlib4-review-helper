Adds the converse of `Module.finitePresentation_of_finite`: a ring $R$ (not necessarily commutative) is (left) Noetherian if and only if every finitely generated $R$-module is finitely presented. Because modules live in a fixed universe, the statement quantifies over modules in any universe $v$ in which $R$ is small.

## isNoetherianRing_iff_forall_finitePresentation

Let $R$ be a ring that is $v$-small. Then $R$ is Noetherian if and only if every finitely generated $R$-module $M$ (with $M$ in universe $v$) is finitely presented.

**Proof.** ($\Rightarrow$) is `Module.finitePresentation_of_finite`. ($\Leftarrow$) Show every left ideal $I$ is finitely generated: $R/I$ is finitely generated, hence (transported to a $v$-small copy via `Shrink`) finitely presented; the kernel of a surjection from $R$ onto a finitely presented module is finitely generated (`Module.FinitePresentation.fg_ker`), and the kernel of $R \to R/I$ is $I$.
