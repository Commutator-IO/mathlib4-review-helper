Defines Frobenius algebras: a (possibly non-unital, non-associative) $R$-algebra $A$ with a chosen linear functional $\varepsilon : A \to R$ such that $a \mapsto (b \mapsto \varepsilon(ab))$ is a bijection $A \cong A^* = \operatorname{Hom}_R(A, R)$. Basic API: the isomorphism with the dual and nondegeneracy of the form; constructors from an associative isomorphism $A \cong A^*$ or from a left-separating associative bilinear form; finite-dimensionality over a field; the Nakayama automorphism; matrix algebras with the trace. A second file connects this to the existing `Coalgebra.IsFrobenius`. Over a field, a Frobenius algebra carries a coalgebra structure (not an instance) satisfying the Frobenius equations. Conversely, an algebra with a coalgebra structure satisfying them is a Frobenius algebra with $\varepsilon$ the counit. Three nondegeneracy lemmas move from `Coalgebra/IsFrobenius.lean` to the new file and are reproved from the general ones.

## FrobeniusAlgebra

Class: for a commutative semiring $R$ and a non-unital non-associative semiring $A$ that is an $R$-module with `SMulCommClass R A A` and `IsScalarTower R A A`, a Frobenius structure is a linear functional `dual` $= \varepsilon : A \to R$ such that $a \mapsto \varepsilon(a\,\cdot)$, $A \to A^*$, is bijective.

**Definition.** Structure with the data `dual` and the proof `bijective_compr₂_mul`. Note that $\varepsilon$ is data, so a given algebra can carry several Frobenius structures.

**Check.** The module docstring writes `(mul R a).compr₂ dual` (lowercase `a`, should be `A`).

## equivDual

The $R$-linear isomorphism $A \cong A^*$, $a \mapsto (b \mapsto \varepsilon(ab))$.

**Definition.** `LinearEquiv.ofBijective` applied to the bijectivity field.

## equivDual_apply

Simp lemma (low priority): `equivDual R A a = (mul R A).compr₂ dual a`.

**Proof.** `rfl`.

## equivDual_apply_apply

$\mathrm{equivDual}(a)(b) = \varepsilon(ab)$.

**Proof.** `simp`.

## toLinearMap_equivDual

Simp lemma: the underlying linear map of `equivDual` is `(mul R A).compr₂ dual`.

**Proof.** `rfl`.

## dual_apply_symm_equivDual_mul

Simp lemma: for $f \in A^*$ and $a \in A$, $\varepsilon(\mathrm{equivDual}^{-1}(f)\,a) = f(a)$.

**Proof.** Rewrite with `equivDual_apply_apply` and `simp`.

## symm_equivDual_compr₂_mul_dual

Simp lemma: $\mathrm{equivDual}^{-1}(b \mapsto \varepsilon(ab)) = a$.

**Proof.** `simp [equivDual]`.

## forall_dual_mul_left_eq_zero_iff

$(\forall b,\ \varepsilon(ab) = 0) \iff a = 0$.

**Proof.** Injectivity of `equivDual`.

## forall_dual_mul_right_eq_zero_iff

$(\forall b,\ \varepsilon(ba) = 0) \iff a = 0$. No associativity is needed.

**Proof.** By the left version it suffices that $\varepsilon(ax) = 0$ for all $x$. Apply the hypothesis to $b = \mathrm{equivDual}^{-1}(y \mapsto \varepsilon(yx))$ and use `dual_apply_symm_equivDual_mul` (surjectivity).

## nondegenerate_equivDual

The bilinear form $(a, b) \mapsto \varepsilon(ab)$ (i.e. `equivDual` as a bilinear map) is nondegenerate: separating on both sides.

**Proof.** The two previous lemmas.

## instance

$R$ is a Frobenius algebra over itself with $\varepsilon = \mathrm{id}$.

**Definition.** Injectivity: evaluate at $1$. Surjectivity: $f$ is the image of $f(1)$.

## ofLinearEquiv

For a non-associative unital $R$-algebra $A$ and an $R$-linear isomorphism $\sigma : A \cong A^*$ with $\sigma(ab)(c) = \sigma(a)(bc)$: the Frobenius structure with $\varepsilon(a) = \sigma(a)(1)$.

**Definition.** Bijectivity: $\varepsilon(ab) = \sigma(ab)(1) = \sigma(a)(b)$, so the map is $\sigma$ itself (`convert σ.bijective`). An `abbrev`, not an instance.

## ofBilinForm

For a field $K$ and a finite-dimensional $K$-algebra $A$ (a ring): a left-separating bilinear form $\sigma$ on $A$ with $\sigma(ab, c) = \sigma(a, bc)$ gives a Frobenius structure with $\varepsilon(a) = \sigma(a, 1)$.

**Definition.** `ofLinearEquiv` applied to $\sigma : A \to A^*$, which is injective (left separating) and hence bijective since $\dim A = \dim A^*$.

## instFiniteDimensional

Instance: a Frobenius algebra over a field $K$ (a ring $A$) is finite-dimensional.

**Proof.** A vector space isomorphic to its dual is finite-dimensional (`Basis.linearEquiv_dual_iff_finiteDimensional`), applied to `equivDual`.

## nakayamaAlgEquiv

For an associative unital $R$-algebra $A$ that is reflexive as a module and Frobenius: the Nakayama automorphism $\nu : A \cong A$ (an $R$-algebra automorphism), characterised by $\varepsilon(\nu(b)\,a) = \varepsilon(ab)$.

**Definition.** As a linear map, $\nu = \mathrm{equivDual}^{-1} \circ \mathrm{equivDual}^{\mathrm{flip}}$, i.e. $\nu(b)$ represents the functional $a \mapsto \varepsilon(ab)$. Unitality and multiplicativity are checked through injectivity of `equivDual`, using associativity. `Module.IsReflexive` is needed for `LinearEquiv.flip`.

## dual_nakayamaAlgEquiv_mul

Simp lemma: $\varepsilon(\nu(b)\,a) = \varepsilon(ab)$.

**Proof.** Unfold the definition, `simp`.

## dual_nakayamaAlgEquiv

Simp lemma: $\varepsilon(\nu(a)) = \varepsilon(a)$.

**Proof.** The previous lemma with $a = 1$.

## algEquiv_eq_nakayamaAlgEquiv_iff

An algebra automorphism $f$ of $A$ equals $\nu$ iff $\varepsilon(f(b)\,a) = \varepsilon(ab)$ for all $a, b$.

**Proof.** Forward by `dual_nakayamaAlgEquiv_mul`. Backward by injectivity of $a \mapsto \varepsilon(a\,\cdot)$.

## _root_.Matrix.frobeniusAlgebra

For a commutative semiring $R$ and a finite type $n$: $M_n(R)$ is a Frobenius algebra with $\varepsilon = \operatorname{tr}$ (not an instance).

**Definition.** Injectivity: $\operatorname{tr}(AX) = \operatorname{tr}(BX)$ for all $X$ forces $A = B$ (`ext_iff_trace_mul_right`). Surjectivity: $f$ is represented by the matrix with entries $(i,j) \mapsto f(E_{ji})$, expanding $X$ in matrix units.

## _root_.Matrix.frobeniusAlgebraDual_eq_traceLinearMap

With the `Matrix.frobeniusAlgebra` structure, `dual` on $M_n(R)$ is `traceLinearMap`.

**Proof.** `rfl`.

**Check.** The name does not follow the usual pattern; something like `Matrix.frobeniusAlgebra_dual` (or `dual_frobeniusAlgebra`) would be more standard.

## comulLeft

For a Frobenius algebra $A$ over a field $K$ (an associative ring): with $\sum_i b_i \otimes b_i^*$ the coevaluation (dual bases) and $b_i^\vee = \mathrm{equivDual}^{-1}(b_i^*)$, the comultiplication $\Delta_L(a) = \sum_i b_i \otimes b_i^\vee a$.

**Definition.** $(\mathrm{id} \otimes \mu) \circ \alpha \circ ((\mathrm{id} \otimes \mathrm{equivDual}^{-1}) \circ \mathrm{coev} \otimes \mathrm{id}) \circ \lambda^{-1}$, as an `abbrev`.

## comulRight

$\Delta_R(a) = \sum_i a b_i \otimes b_i^\vee$.

**Definition.** The mirror image: $(\mu \otimes \mathrm{id}) \circ \alpha^{-1} \circ (\mathrm{id} \otimes (\ldots \circ \mathrm{coev})) \circ \rho^{-1}$.

## lTensor_dual_comp_comulLeft_comp_lid

$(\mathrm{id}_A \otimes \varepsilon) \circ \Delta_L \circ \lambda = \mathrm{comm} : K \otimes A \to A \otimes K$. That is, $\sum_i b_i\, \varepsilon(b_i^\vee a) = a$: the right counit law for $\Delta_L$.

**Proof.** A `calc`: rewrite as contraction after coevaluation, then the zig-zag identity `contractLeft_assoc_coevaluation'`.

## rTensor_comp_comulRight_comp_rid

$(\varepsilon \otimes \mathrm{id}_A) \circ \Delta_R \circ \rho = \mathrm{comm} : A \otimes K \to K \otimes A$. That is, $\sum_i \varepsilon(a b_i)\, b_i^\vee = a$.

**Proof.** A `calc` conjugating by `equivDual`, then the zig-zag identity `contractLeft_assoc_coevaluation`.

**Check.** The name omits `dual`, unlike its twin `lTensor_dual_comp_comulLeft_comp_lid`. `rTensor_dual_comp_comulRight_comp_rid` would be consistent.

## comulLeft_eq_comulRight

$\Delta_L = \Delta_R$, i.e. $\sum_i b_i \otimes b_i^\vee a = \sum_i a b_i \otimes b_i^\vee$.

**Proof.** Write $\sum_i b_i \otimes b_i^\vee$ as a finite sum over a finset $S$. A four-step `calc` expands both sides with the two counit identities above, using associativity and `Finset.sum_comm`. A fairly long, hand-guided computation (it contains `simp_rw +singlePass` and a closing `rfl` after `simp`).

## toCoalgebra

A Frobenius algebra over a field becomes a $K$-coalgebra with $\Delta = \Delta_L$ and counit $\varepsilon$ (an `abbrev`, not an instance, since $A$ may carry other coalgebra structures).

**Definition.** Coassociativity uses $\Delta_L = \Delta_R$ on one side and a sum expansion. The counit laws are the two lemmas above (with $\Delta_L = \Delta_R$ for the left one).

## isFrobenius_toCoalgebra

With `toCoalgebra`, $A$ satisfies the Frobenius equations (`Coalgebra.IsFrobenius K A`).

**Proof.** Rewrite one $\Delta_L$ as $\Delta_R$, then `ext` and `simp` on the coevaluation sums.

## ofIsFrobenius

For a commutative semiring $R$ and a non-associative unital $R$-algebra $A$ with an $R$-coalgebra structure satisfying the Frobenius equations: the Frobenius algebra structure with $\varepsilon$ the counit (an `abbrev`).

**Definition.** Bijectivity is the existing `IsFrobenius.compr₂_mul_counit_bijective`.

## ofIsFrobenius_dual_eq

With `ofIsFrobenius`, `dual` is the counit.

**Proof.** `rfl`.
