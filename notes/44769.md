New file computing two volumes. The first is the volume of a solid of revolution, $\pi\int_a^b f^2$. The second is the volume of a cone with arbitrary measurable base, $\mu(B)\cdot h/n$ in dimension $n$, with the round cone $\pi R^2 H/3$ as a special case. The ambient space for the concrete results is $\mathbb R \times \mathrm{EuclideanSpace}\,\mathbb R\,(\mathrm{Fin}\,2)$ with the product measure (not $\mathbb R^3$ = `EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin 3)`). The tools are `Measure.prod_apply` (Cavalieri), `convexJoin` for cones, `addHaar_image_linearMap`, `addHaar_smul` and `volume_closedBall_fin_two`.

## prod_setOf_mem_closedBall

**Cavalieri for ball slices.** Let $\mu$ be a measure on $\alpha$, $\nu$ an s-finite measure on a (pseudo)metric space $E$ with opens measurable, $s \subseteq \alpha$ measurable, $r : \alpha \to \mathbb R$ measurable and $c \in E$. Then $(\mu\otimes\nu)\{(x,y) : x \in s,\ y \in \bar B(c, r(x))\} = \int_s \nu(\bar B(c,r(x)))\,d\mu(x)$.

**Proof.** Show the set is measurable, then apply `prod_apply` and identify the slices with an indicator.

## image_convexJoin_singleton

Private: an affine map $f$ sends the cone $\mathrm{convexJoin}(\{x\}, s)$ to $\mathrm{convexJoin}(\{f x\}, f(s))$.

## convexJoin_zero_prod

Private: for $h > 0$, the cone with apex $0$ over $\{h\}\times B \subseteq \mathbb R \times F$ equals $\{(t,y) : 0 \le t \le h,\ y \in (t/h)\,B\}$.

## volume_prod_convexJoin_zero_prod

Private: for a measurable $B \subseteq F$ and an additive Haar measure $\mu$ on $F$, the cone with apex $0$ over $\{1\}\times B$ has $(\mathrm{Leb}\otimes\mu)$-measure $\mu(B)/(\dim F + 1)$.

**Proof.** The slice at height $t \in [0,1]$ is $tB$, with measure $t^{\dim F}\mu(B)$. Integrate over $[0,1]$.

## volume_prod_convexJoin_singleton_prod

**Volume of a cone.** Let $F$ be a finite-dimensional real normed space with additive Haar measure $\mu$, $B \subseteq F$ measurable, $a \in \mathbb R\times F$ and $c \in \mathbb R$. The cone with apex $a$ over the base $\{c\}\times B$ has $(\mathrm{Leb}\otimes\mu)$-measure $\mu(B)\cdot |c - a_1|/(\dim F + 1)$.

**Proof.** The cone is the image of the standard cone (apex $0$, base $\{1\}\times(B - a_2)$) under the linear map $(t,y)\mapsto((c-a_1)t, y)$, which has determinant $c - a_1$, followed by translation by $a$. Combine `addHaar_image_linearMap`, translation invariance and the private lemma.

## volume_setOf_mem_norm_le

For measurable $s \subseteq \mathbb R$ and measurable $r : \mathbb R \to \mathbb R$, the set $\{(x,y) \in \mathbb R\times\mathbb R^2 : x \in s,\ \|y\| \le r(x)\}$ has volume $\int_s \pi\, r(x)_+^2\,dx$ (as a lower Lebesgue integral in $[0,\infty]$).

**Proof.** Apply `prod_setOf_mem_closedBall` with the area of a disc.

**Check.** No docstring.

## volume_solidOfRevolution

Let $a \le b$ and $f$ be continuous and nonnegative on $[a,b]$. The solid $\{(x,y) \in \mathbb R\times\mathbb R^2 : a \le x \le b,\ \|y\| \le f(x)\}$ has volume $\pi\int_a^b f(x)^2\,dx$ (as an `ENNReal.ofReal`).

**Proof.** Replace $f$ by the measurable function equal to $f$ on $[a,b]$ and $0$ elsewhere. Then apply the previous lemma and convert the lower integral to a Bochner integral, using integrability of $f^2$ on the compact interval.

**Check.** `hab : a ≤ b` looks unnecessary. If $b < a$, the set is empty and $\int_a^b f^2 \le 0$, so `ENNReal.ofReal` gives $0$ too.

## volume_cone

For $R \ge 0$ and $H > 0$, the round cone $\{(t,y) \in \mathbb R\times\mathbb R^2 : 0 \le t \le H,\ \|y\| \le Rt/H\}$ (apex at the origin, base a disc of radius $R$ at height $H$) has volume $\pi R^2 H/3$.

**Proof.** Identify the set with the cone with apex $0$ over $\{H\}\times\bar B(0,R)$ (`convexJoin_zero_prod`). Apply `volume_prod_convexJoin_singleton_prod` with $\dim\mathbb R^2 = 2$ and area $\pi R^2$ of the disc.
