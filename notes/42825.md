Proves the derivative recurrences for the Bessel function of the first kind $J_a$ (DLMF 10.6.1–10.6.2): $xJ_a' = aJ_a - xJ_{a+1} = xJ_{a-1} - aJ_a$ and $2J_a' = J_{a-1} - J_{a+1}$, on the slit plane for complex $a$ and on all of $\mathbb{C}$ for integer $a$. The key input is a new formula for the derivative of the regularized generalized hypergeometric function, $\frac{d}{dz}\,\mathbf{F}(a;b;z) = (\prod a_i)\,\mathbf{F}(a+1;b+1;z)$, proved via a coefficient identity. Also tags `AnalyticOnNhd.deriv` with `@[fun_prop]`.

## mul_deriv_besselJ_eq_besselJ_add_one

For $a \in \mathbb{C}$ and $x$ in the slit plane $\mathbb{C}\setminus(-\infty,0]$: $x\,J_a'(x) = a\,J_a(x) - x\,J_{a+1}(x)$.

**Proof.** Unfold $J_a(x) = (x/2)^a\,\mathbf{F}_{0,1}(a+1; -(x/2)^2)$, apply the product and chain rules, use `deriv_regularizedHGFun` (radius is infinite) for the hypergeometric factor and `deriv_cpow_const` for $(x/2)^a$, then simplify powers with `grind`.

## mul_deriv_besselJ_eq_besselJ_sub_one

For $a \in \mathbb{C}$ and $x$ in the slit plane: $x\,J_a'(x) = x\,J_{a-1}(x) - a\,J_a(x)$.

**Proof.** Linear combination of the previous lemma and the existing recurrence $2aJ_a = xJ_{a-1} + xJ_{a+1}$ (`two_mul_self_mul_besselJ`).

## two_mul_deriv_besselJ

For $a \in \mathbb{C}$ and $x$ in the slit plane: $2J_a'(x) = J_{a-1}(x) - J_{a+1}(x)$.

**Proof.** $x \neq 0$; multiply by $x$ and add the two previous identities.

## two_mul_deriv_besselJ_int

For $a \in \mathbb{Z}$ and every $x \in \mathbb{C}$: $2J_a'(x) = J_{a-1}(x) - J_{a+1}(x)$.

**Proof.** Both sides are entire for integer orders; they agree on the open slit plane (previous lemma), hence frequently near $1$, so they agree everywhere by the identity theorem `AnalyticOnNhd.eq_of_frequently_eq`.

## mul_deriv_besselJ_eq_besselJ_add_one_int

For $a \in \mathbb{Z}$ and every $x \in \mathbb{C}$: $x\,J_a'(x) = a\,J_a(x) - x\,J_{a+1}(x)$.

**Proof.** Linear combination of `two_mul_deriv_besselJ_int` and `two_mul_self_mul_besselJ`.

## mul_deriv_besselJ_eq_besselJ_sub_one_int

For $a \in \mathbb{Z}$ and every $x \in \mathbb{C}$: $x\,J_a'(x) = x\,J_{a-1}(x) - a\,J_a(x)$.

**Proof.** Linear combination of the previous lemma and `two_mul_self_mul_besselJ`.

## mul_regularizedHGFunCoeff

For multisets $a, b$ of complex parameters and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, with $C_{a,b}(n) = \prod_i (a_i)_n \,/\, (n!\,\prod_j \Gamma(b_j+n))$ the regularized hypergeometric coefficients:
$$(n+1)\,C_{a,b}(n+1) = \Big(\prod_i a_i\Big)\, C_{a+1,\,b+1}(n),$$
where $a+1$, $b+1$ add $1$ to every parameter.

**Proof.** Unfold; $(n+1)! = (n+1)\,n!$, and $(x)_{n+1} = x\,(x+1)_n$ (definition of `ascPochhammer`), then `grind` matches $\Gamma(b_j + (n+1))$ with $\Gamma((b_j+1)+n)$.

## summable_pow_smul_coeff_derivSeries_regularizedHGFunSeries

For $z$ strictly inside the radius of convergence of the regularized hypergeometric series, the series $\sum_n z^n\, c_n$ is summable, where $c_n$ are the scalar coefficients of the derivative series (`derivSeries`).

**Proof.** The derivative series has radius at least that of the original (`radius_le_radius_derivSeries`), so `FormalMultilinearSeries.summable` applies.

## deriv_regularizedHGFun

For $z$ strictly inside the radius of convergence of the regularized series:
$$\frac{d}{dz}\,\mathbf{F}(a;b;z) = \Big(\prod_i a_i\Big)\,\mathbf{F}(a+1;\,b+1;\,z).$$

**Proof.** Differentiate the power series termwise (`FormalMultilinearSeries.fderiv_sum`), rewrite the derivative series' coefficients as $(n+1)C_{a,b}(n+1)$ (using the summability lemma to evaluate the `tsum` of linear maps), then apply `mul_regularizedHGFunCoeff` and pull out the constant.
