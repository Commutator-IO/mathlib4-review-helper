Characterises, over a commutative ring $R$, the polynomials coprime to a linear factor $X - a$: they are those whose value at $a$ is a unit. The existing one-way lemma `isCoprime_X_sub_C_of_isUnit_sub` becomes an iff (`isCoprime_X_sub_C_iff_isUnit_sub`, the old name kept as a deprecated alias) and is moved from the `IsDomain` part of the file to the general `CommRing` section; the two call sites are updated.

## isCoprime_X_sub_C_iff

For a commutative ring $R$, $a \in R$ and $p \in R[X]$: $X - a$ and $p$ are coprime (there are $u, v$ with $u(X-a) + vp = 1$) if and only if $p(a)$ is a unit of $R$.

**Proof.** ($\Rightarrow$) Apply evaluation at $a$, a ring hom, which preserves coprimality; $0$ and $p(a)$ coprime means $p(a)$ is a unit. ($\Leftarrow$) Write $p = (X-a)q + p(a)$ (from `X_sub_C_dvd_sub_C_eval`); since the constant $p(a)$ is a unit, $X - a$ is coprime to $p$.

## isCoprime_X_sub_C_iff_isUnit_sub

For a commutative ring $R$ and $a, b \in R$: $X - a$ and $X - b$ are coprime iff $a - b$ is a unit. Tagged `@[simp]`.

**Proof.** `simp` with `isCoprime_X_sub_C_iff`, since $(X-b)(a) = a - b$.

**Check.** The deprecated alias `isCoprime_X_sub_C_of_isUnit_sub` has the same type as the old lemma up to binder order, so the `+typeChanged` flag (and maybe the deprecation itself) may be unnecessary.

## isCoprime_X_iff

For a commutative ring $R$ and $p \in R[X]$: $X$ and $p$ are coprime iff the constant coefficient $p_0 = p(0)$ is a unit.

**Proof.** The case $a = 0$ of `isCoprime_X_sub_C_iff`, using `coeff_zero_eq_eval_zero`.

## isCoprime_X_sub_C_of_isUnit_sub

Removed as a theorem: "if $a - b$ is a unit then $X - a$ and $X - b$ are coprime" is now the reverse direction of `isCoprime_X_sub_C_iff_isUnit_sub`, and the old name survives as a deprecated alias (see that section).

## pairwise_coprime_X_sub_C

Statement unchanged: for an injective family $s : I \to K$ in a field, the polynomials $X - s_i$ are pairwise coprime.

**Proof.** Updated to use `isCoprime_X_sub_C_iff_isUnit_sub.2` instead of the removed lemma.

## Polynomial.mul_star_dvd_of_aeval_eq_zero_im_ne_zero

Statement unchanged (a real polynomial with a non-real root $z$ is divisible by $(X - \bar z)(X - z)$ over $\mathbb C$); only the call to the removed lemma is replaced by `isCoprime_X_sub_C_iff_isUnit_sub.2`.
