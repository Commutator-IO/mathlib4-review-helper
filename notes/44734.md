Proves that a commutative semilocal ring (finitely many maximal ideals, expressed as `Finite (MaximalSpectrum R)`) has stable range one, a result of Bass. Builds on the Chinese remainder theorem for pairwise coprime ideals (`Ideal.exists_forall_sub_mem_ideal`). Adds the reference `bass1964`.

## IsCoprime.exists_isUnit_add_mul

Let $R$ be a commutative ring with finitely many maximal ideals. If $a, b \in R$ are coprime ($ua + vb = 1$ for some $u, v$), then there exists $t \in R$ such that $a + tb$ is a unit.

**Proof.** For each maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$ choose a target $x_{\mathfrak m}$: $0$ if $b \in \mathfrak m$, otherwise $b^{-1}(1-a)$ modulo $\mathfrak m$; by CRT over the finitely many pairwise coprime maximal ideals pick $t \equiv x_{\mathfrak m} \pmod{\mathfrak m}$ for all $\mathfrak m$. If $a + tb$ were a non-unit it would lie in some maximal $\mathfrak m$; if $b \in \mathfrak m$ then $a \in \mathfrak m$, contradicting coprimality, and otherwise $1 \in \mathfrak m$ by an explicit `linear_combination` identity.
