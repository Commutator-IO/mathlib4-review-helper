New file on integer-valued polynomials: defines the $\mathbb{Z}$-subalgebra $\operatorname{Int}(\mathbb{Z}) \subseteq \mathbb{Q}[X]$ of polynomials with $P(\mathbb{Z}) \subseteq \mathbb{Z}$ and proves Pólya's theorem that the binomial polynomials $\binom{X}{n}$ (`Ring.choose X n`) form a $\mathbb{Z}$-basis. Along the way it proves Newton's forward difference formula over a field of characteristic zero, building on `Mathlib.Algebra.Group.ForwardDiff` and the binomial ring structure (`Ring.choose`).

## eval_choose_X

For a field $K$ of characteristic zero, $a \in K$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, evaluating the polynomial $\binom{X}{n} \in K[X]$ at $a$ gives $\binom{a}{n} = \frac{a(a-1)\cdots(a-n+1)}{n!}$ (`Ring.choose a n`).

**Proof.** `Ring.map_choose` applied to the evaluation ring hom at $a$.

## eval_natCast_choose_X

For $K$ as above and $m, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\binom{X}{n}(m) = \binom{m}{n}$ (the natural binomial coefficient, cast into $K$).

**Proof.** `eval_choose_X` and `Ring.choose_natCast`.

## eq_sum_fwdDiff_iter_smul_choose

Newton's forward difference formula: for a field $K$ of characteristic zero and $P \in K[X]$,
$$P = \sum_{k=0}^{\deg P} (\Delta^k P)(0)\,\binom{X}{k},$$
where $\Delta f(x) = f(x+1) - f(x)$ is applied to the function $x \mapsto P(x)$ and $\deg$ is `natDegree`.

**Proof.** Both sides agree at the infinitely many points $m \in \mathbb{N} \subseteq K$: at $m$, `shift_eq_sum_fwdDiff_iter` gives $P(m) = \sum_{k\le m}\binom{m}{k}(\Delta^kP)(0)$; both ranges are extended to $m + \deg P$, using $\Delta^k P = 0$ for $k > \deg P$ and $\binom{m}{k} = 0$ for $k > m$.

## intValued

**Definition.** The $\mathbb{Z}$-subalgebra of $\mathbb{Q}[X]$ consisting of the $P$ with $P(n) \in \mathbb{Z}$ for every $n \in \mathbb{Z}$; closure under $+$, $\times$ and containing the integer constants are checked pointwise.

## mem_intValued

$P \in \operatorname{Int}(\mathbb{Z})$ iff $P(n) \in \mathbb{Z}$ for all $n \in \mathbb{Z}$.

**Proof.** Definitional (`Iff.rfl`).

## choose_X_mem_intValued

For every $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\binom{X}{n} \in \mathbb{Q}[X]$ is integer-valued.

**Proof.** At $m \in \mathbb{Z}$ its value is `Ring.choose m n` computed in $\mathbb{Z}$, transported by `Ring.map_choose` along $\mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{Q}$.

## exists_eq_sum_intCast_smul_choose

Pólya: if $P \in \mathbb{Q}[X]$ takes integer values at all natural numbers, then there are integers $c_k$ with $P = \sum_{k=0}^{\deg P} c_k \binom{X}{k}$.

**Proof.** Take $c_k = (\Delta^k f)(0)$, where $f : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{Z}$ records the integer values $P(m)$; Newton's formula, with $\Delta^k$ expanded as a finite alternating sum of shifts, shows these are the coefficients.

## mem_intValued_iff_forall_natCast

$P \in \mathbb{Q}[X]$ is integer-valued on $\mathbb{Z}$ iff it is integer-valued on $\mathbb{N}$.

**Proof.** Forward direction by casting; backward direction from Pólya's expansion, since each $\binom{X}{k}$ is in the subalgebra.

## linearIndependent_choose_X

The polynomials $\binom{X}{n}$, $n \in \mathbb{N}$, are linearly independent over $\mathbb{Z}$ in $\mathbb{Q}[X]$.

**Proof.** If $\sum_{k\in s} g_k\binom{X}{k} = 0$, then $m \mapsto \sum_k g_k\binom{m}{k}$ vanishes on $\mathbb{N}$; applying $\Delta^i$ at $0$ and `fwdDiff_iter_choose_zero` ($\Delta^i\binom{\cdot}{k}(0) = [i = k]$) gives $g_i = 0$.

## intValuedBasis

**Definition.** The $\mathbb{Z}$-basis of $\operatorname{Int}(\mathbb{Z})$ indexed by $\mathbb{N}$ whose $n$-th vector is $\binom{X}{n}$, built with `Basis.mk` from linear independence and spanning (Pólya's expansion).

## coe_intValuedBasis

The $n$-th basis vector of `intValuedBasis`, viewed in $\mathbb{Q}[X]$, is $\binom{X}{n}$ (simp lemma).

**Proof.** `simp` unfolding the definition.
