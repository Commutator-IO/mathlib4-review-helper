Adds a new file `Analysis/Matrix/GNS.lean`. It defines the *weight* of a linear functional $f$ on $n\times n$ matrices: the matrix $W$ with $f(a) = \operatorname{tr}(aW)$. It then shows two things. First, $f$ is star-preserving iff $W$ is Hermitian. Second, $f$ is monotone and star-preserving iff $W$ is positive semidefinite; over $\mathbb{C}$, monotone alone is enough. So positive functionals on $M_n(\mathbb{C})$ correspond to PSD matrices, which is the setting of the GNS inner product $\langle x, y\rangle = \operatorname{tr}(yQx^*)$. The existing `toMatrixInnerProductSpace` constructions move unchanged from `Analysis/Matrix/Order.lean` into the new file.

## Module.Dual.weight

**Definition.** For a semiring $R$ and an $R$-linear functional $f$ on $M_n(R)$, define $W = \sum_{i,j} f(E_{ij})\,E_{ji}$, i.e. $W_{ji} = f(E_{ij})$.

**Check.** All the `Module.Dual.*` declarations here sit inside `namespace Matrix`, so their full names are `Matrix.Module.Dual.weight` and so on. That is probably not intended: they should be `_root_.Module.Dual.*` or moved out of the namespace. Also check that `f.weight` dot notation resolves outside the namespace. Separately, the module docstring says the file "shows that the GNS-inner product space on matrices is equivalent to `Matrix.toMatrixInnerProductSpace`", but no such statement is proved.

## Module.Dual.eq_trace_mul_weight

For every matrix $a$, $f(a) = \operatorname{tr}(a\,W)$, where $W$ is the weight of $f$.

**Proof.** Expand $a = \sum a_{ij}E_{ij}$, use linearity, and compute the trace with `simp`.

## Module.Dual.isSelfAdjoint_toConv_iff_isHermitian_weight

For a commutative star semiring $R$, $f$ is self-adjoint for the intrinsic star on linear maps, i.e. $f(a^*) = \overline{f(a)}$ for all $a$, iff its weight $W$ is Hermitian.

**Proof.** Rewrite both sides with `eq_trace_mul_weight` and $\operatorname{tr}(x^*) = \overline{\operatorname{tr} x}$. Then use cyclicity and `ext_iff_trace_mul_right`: $\operatorname{tr}(aW) = \operatorname{tr}(aW^*)$ for all $a$ forces $W = W^*$.

## Module.Dual.monotone_and_isSelfAdjoint_toConv_iff_posSemidef_weight

For $\mathbb{K} = \mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and a linear functional $f$ on $M_n(\mathbb{K})$ (with the Loewner order), the following are equivalent:
- $f$ is monotone and star-preserving;
- the weight of $f$ is positive semidefinite.

**Proof.** Star-preserving ⇔ Hermitian by the previous lemma. Then use $v^*Wv = \operatorname{tr}(W\,vv^*)$. Monotone gives $v^*Wv = f(vv^*) \ge 0$. Conversely, write $y - x \ge 0$ as $\sum_k v_kv_k^*$ and sum.

**Check.** No docstring on this main theorem, only a `--` comment. The comment reads "(but a lot of missing API ugh)".

## isHermitian_iff_star_dotProduct

A complex square matrix $A$ is Hermitian iff $x^*Ax$ is real, i.e. $\overline{x^*Ax} = x^*Ax$, for every $x \in \mathbb{C}^n$.

**Proof.** Go to the Euclidean-space linear map. Hermitian ⇔ symmetric, and symmetric ⇔ $\langle Ax, x\rangle$ real (`LinearMap.isSymmetric_iff_inner_map_self_real`).

**Check.** It is specific to $\mathbb{C}$, but the name does not say so. It also rebinds `{n} [Fintype n]`, shadowing the section variable.

## posSemidef_iff_complex

A complex square matrix $x$ is PSD iff $0 \le y^*xy$ (in the order on $\mathbb{C}$) for every $y$. Hermitianity is not assumed separately.

**Proof.** Combine `posSemidef_iff_dotProduct_mulVec` with the previous lemma. Nonnegative in $\mathbb{C}$ means real and $\ge 0$.

**Check.** The name `posSemidef_iff_complex` is not descriptive. Something like `posSemidef_iff_dotProduct_mulVec_nonneg_of_complex` would say what the lemma states.

## Module.Dual.monotone_iff_posSemidef_weight

A linear functional $f$ on $M_n(\mathbb{C})$ is monotone iff its weight is PSD. Over $\mathbb{C}$ the star-preserving condition is automatic.

**Proof.** Rewrite with `posSemidef_iff_complex`, then repeat the argument of `monotone_and_isSelfAdjoint_toConv_iff_posSemidef_weight` word for word.
