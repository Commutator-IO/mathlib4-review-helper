For preadditive categories $C, D$, shows that a functor $\mathrm{Kar}(C) \to D$ out of the Karoubi envelope is additive as soon as its restriction along $C \to \mathrm{Kar}(C)$ is, and deduces that, when $D$ is idempotent complete, the existing equivalence `karoubiUniversal C D` restricts to an equivalence of categories of additive functors $C \to^+ D \simeq \mathrm{Kar}(C) \to^+ D$. Builds on `whiskeringLeftObjToKaroubiFullyFaithful` and the `AdditiveFunctor` category `C ⥤+ D`.

## Karoubi.hom_eq_decompId_i_comp

For objects $P = (X, p)$, $Q = (Y, q)$ of the Karoubi envelope and a morphism $f : P \to Q$, $f$ factors as $P \xrightarrow{i_P} (X, \mathrm{id}) \xrightarrow{f.f} (Y, \mathrm{id}) \xrightarrow{p_Q} Q$, where $i_P$, $p_Q$ are the canonical maps `decompId_i`, `decompId_p` and the middle map is the underlying morphism $f.f : X \to Y$ viewed in $C$.

**Proof.** Extensionality and `simp`.

## additive_of_toKaroubi_comp

For preadditive $C, D$ and a functor $F : \mathrm{Kar}(C) \to D$, if $F \circ \iota_C$ is additive (where $\iota_C : C \to \mathrm{Kar}(C)$), then $F$ is additive.

**Proof.** By the factorization above, $F(h) = F(i_P) \circ (F\iota_C)(h.f) \circ F(p_Q)$ for every $h : P \to Q$; since $(f+g).f = f.f + g.f$ and $F\iota_C$ is additive, bilinearity of composition gives $F(f+g) = F f + F g$.

## additive_iff_toKaroubi_comp

For preadditive $C, D$, a functor $F : \mathrm{Kar}(C) \to D$ is additive if and only if $F \circ \iota_C$ is additive.

**Proof.** Forward: composite of additive functors (instance). Backward: `additive_of_toKaroubi_comp`.

## whiskeringLeftToKaroubiAdditive

**Definition.** The functor $(\mathrm{Kar}(C) \to^+ D) \to (C \to^+ D)$, $F \mapsto F \circ \iota_C$, i.e. precomposition with $\iota_C$ restricted to additive functors (lifted through the full subcategory of additive functors).

## whiskeringLeftToKaroubiAdditiveFullyFaithful

The functor `whiskeringLeftToKaroubiAdditive C D` is fully faithful (as `Functor.FullyFaithful` data).

**Definition.** Its composite with the (faithful) forgetful functor to all functors $C \to D$ equals inclusion followed by the fully faithful precomposition `whiskeringLeftObjToKaroubiFullyFaithful`; conclude with `FullyFaithful.ofCompFaithful`.

## karoubiUniversalAdditive

For preadditive $C, D$ with $D$ idempotent complete, an equivalence of categories $(C \to^+ D) \simeq (\mathrm{Kar}(C) \to^+ D)$ between additive functors.

**Definition.** The inverse of `whiskeringLeftToKaroubiAdditive C D`, which is an equivalence: fully faithful as above, and essentially surjective because any additive $G : C \to D$ is, up to iso, $F \circ \iota_C$ for some $F : \mathrm{Kar}(C) \to D$ (by the non-additive equivalence, using idempotent completeness), and such $F$ is additive by `additive_of_toKaroubi_comp`.
