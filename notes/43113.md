Adds `ContinuousLinearMap.smulRightₗ'`: for topological vector spaces (no norms), the map $c \mapsto (x \mapsto (y \mapsto c(y)\,x))$ from the strong dual $E'$ to $\mathcal{L}(F, \mathcal{L}(E, F))$, linear in $c$ and continuous linear in $x$ and $y$. It is the TVS counterpart of the normed, fully continuous `smulRightL`, and the PR records that `smulRightL` is `smulRightₗ'` once continuity in $c$ is forgotten. The `IsTopologicalAddGroup E` and `ContinuousSMul 𝕜 E` assumptions are moved so that they apply only to `toSpanSingletonCLE`.

## toLinearMap_smulRightL

Simp lemma: for seminormed spaces, the continuous trilinear map `smulRightL 𝕜 E Fₗ`, viewed as a plain linear map in its first argument, equals `smulRightₗ' 𝕜 E Fₗ`.

**Proof.** `rfl`.

## smulRightₗ'

For a nontrivially normed field $\mathbb{k}$, a topological $\mathbb{k}$-module $E$ (only `TopologicalSpace E` assumed) and a topological vector space $F$ (topological additive group, continuous scalar multiplication): the $\mathbb{k}$-linear map $E' \to \mathcal{L}(F, \mathcal{L}(E, F))$, $c \mapsto (x \mapsto c(\cdot)\,x)$, with $E'$ the strong dual and $\mathcal{L}$ continuous linear maps.

**Definition.** $c \mapsto$ (precomposition with $c$, i.e. `c.precomp F`) composed with `toSpanSingletonCLE`, the identification $F \cong \mathcal{L}(\mathbb{k}, F)$. Additivity and homogeneity by `ext; simp`. `@[simps! apply_apply_apply]` gives $\mathrm{smulRight}'(c)(x)(y) = c(y)\,x$.

**Check.** The new simp lemma `smulRightₗ'_apply_apply` rewrites a subterm of the LHS of the generated `smulRightₗ'_apply_apply_apply`, so the `simpNF` linter may flag the generated lemma. Confirm the linter is clean or drop one of the two.

## smulRightₗ'_apply_apply

Simp lemma: `smulRightₗ' 𝕜 E F c x = c.smulRight x`, the continuous linear map $y \mapsto c(y)\,x$.

**Proof.** `rfl`.

## toLinearMap_smulRightₗ'_apply

Simp lemma: for $c \in E'$, `smulRightₗ' 𝕜 E F c` with its outer continuity forgotten (a linear map $F \to \mathcal{L}(E, F)$) is the existing `c.smulRightₗ`.

**Proof.** `rfl`.
