Generalises `AlgEquiv` (`A ≃ₐ[R] B`) from unital algebras to non-unital, non-associative ones. Before, an algebra equivalence was a ring isomorphism commuting with `algebraMap R`, for commutative $R$ and `Algebra R A`. Now it is an $R$-linear ring isomorphism: a bijection preserving $+$ and $\times$ with $f(r \cdot a) = r \cdot f(a)$, for a semiring $R$ and $R$-modules $A, B$ that are `NonUnitalNonAssocSemiring`s. For unital algebras over commutative $R$ the two notions agree. The old style of construction survives as the new constructor `AlgEquiv.ofCommutes`. `StarAlgEquiv` now extends `AlgEquiv`, which makes `NonUnitalAlgEquivClass` redundant, so it is deprecated in favour of `AlgEquivClass`. Several constructors lose their now-redundant `map_one` hypotheses (`ofLinearEquiv`, `algEquivOfLinearEquiv(Triple)TensorProduct`). The rest of the ~130 files are adapted to these changes.

## *

Mechanical adaptation. The declaration either:
- builds an `AlgEquiv` with the field `map_smul'` instead of `commutes'`, or goes through `AlgEquiv.ofCommutes e h` instead of `{ e with commutes' := h }` / `AlgEquiv.ofRingEquiv`;
- has its typeclass assumptions generalised from `[CommSemiring R] [Semiring A] [Algebra R A]` to `[Semiring R] [NonUnitalNonAssocSemiring A] [Module R A]` where the proof allows;
- was moved into a section that keeps `Algebra` assumptions because it mentions `algebraMap`, `AlgHom` or `1`;
- or only needed proof tweaks: explicit `(R := R)`/`(Q := Q)` arguments, `Module.Finite.`/`Algebra.` prefixes, removed `map_one` arguments.

Some unused `[DecidableEq _]` hypotheses are also dropped (`piAlgEquiv`, `transposeAlgEquiv`, `toMatrix_reindex'`, `reindexAlgEquiv`, `_root_.Matrix.trace_map`). Statements are mathematically unchanged unless a section below says otherwise.

## AlgEquiv

Structure: for a semiring $R$ and $R$-modules $A, B$ that are (non-unital, non-associative) semirings, an equivalence $A \simeq B$ preserving $+$ and $\times$ such that $f(r \cdot a) = r \cdot f(a)$ for all $r \in R$, $a \in A$. The field `map_smul'` replaces `commutes' : f (algebraMap R A r) = algebraMap R B r`. The parent order is now `A ≃ B, A ≃+ B, A ≃* B, A ≃+* B`.

**Definition.** As stated. The docstring explains the switch and points to `ofCommutes`.

**Check.** Typo in the docstring: "avaiable". $R$ is now any semiring, not necessarily commutative. Make sure that is intended for the notation `A ≃ₐ[R] B`.

## AlgEquivClass

`AlgEquivClass F R A B` now just combines `RingEquivClass F A B` and `MulActionHomClass F R A B` (preserves $r \cdot$), under the same weakened assumptions. The `commutes` field is gone: use `AlgHomClass.commutes` via the `toAlgHomClass` instance.

**Definition.** Class with no new fields (there is an empty `where`).

**Check.** Equiv.lean also adds an anonymous `instance : MulActionHomClass (A₁ ≃ₐ[R] A₂) R A₁ A₂` with the comment `-- needed?`. It looks redundant now that `AlgEquivClass` extends `MulActionHomClass`: either drop it or explain it.

## toAlgHomClass

Instance: under `[CommSemiring R] [Algebra R A] [Algebra R B]`, an `AlgEquivClass` is an `AlgHomClass`.

**Proof.** `commutes` is derived from `map_smul` via `algebraMap r = r • 1`.

## ofCommutes

New constructor: given a ring isomorphism $e : A_1 \simeq A_2$ between $R$-algebras ($R$ commutative) with $e(\mathrm{algebraMap}\, r) = \mathrm{algebraMap}\, r$ for all $r$, the corresponding `A₁ ≃ₐ[R] A₂`.

**Definition.** `map_smul'` comes from `Algebra.smul_def` and the commuting hypothesis. Marked `@[reducible, inline]`.

## commutes

Statement unchanged ($e(\mathrm{algebraMap}_{A_1} r) = \mathrm{algebraMap}_{A_2} r$, simp), but it now carries its own `[CommSemiring R] [Algebra R A₁] [Algebra R A₂]` and is moved before `toAlgHom`.

**Proof.** `AlgHomClass.commutes e`, no longer the structure field.

## ofCommutes_symm_apply

**Proof.** `rfl`.

**Definition.** Same, with `commutes' := e.commutes` supplied explicitly.

**Definition.** Now `e.toLinearEquiv.toLinearMap` instead of going through `toAlgHom`. `toAlgHom_toLinearMap` and `toLinearMap_ofAlgHom` move to a section with `Algebra` assumptions.

## ofLinearEquiv

Upgrade an $R$-linear equivalence $l : A_1 \simeq_R A_2$ that is multiplicative to an algebra equivalence. **Signature change:** the hypothesis `map_one : l 1 = 1` is dropped. It is not needed: a multiplicative bijection automatically sends $1$ to $1$, and the algebras need not be unital any more.

**Definition.** `map_smul'` comes from `map_smul l`.

**Check.** A breaking change in explicit arguments with no deprecation path. Every caller had to drop an argument. This deserves a line in the PR description.

## ofLinearEquiv_symm

The inverse of `ofLinearEquiv l h` is `ofLinearEquiv l.symm h'`, where $h'$ is the multiplicativity of the inverse. Simplified now that there is no `map_one` argument.

**Proof.** `rfl`.

## ofRingEquiv

**Signature change:** the hypothesis on the ring equivalence $f$ is now $\forall r\, x,\ f(r \cdot x) = r \cdot f(x)$ instead of $f(\mathrm{algebraMap}\, r) = \mathrm{algebraMap}\, r$. The old behaviour is now `ofCommutes`.

**Definition.** `map_smul' := hf`.

**Check.** Same name, different hypothesis, no deprecation. Callers that passed the `algebraMap` hypothesis now fail with a type error rather than a deprecation warning. All in-tree callers were switched to `ofCommutes`.

## StarAlgEquiv

Structure: a ⋆-algebra equivalence now extends both `A ≃⋆+* B` and `A ≃ₐ[R] B`. It needs `[Semiring R]`, `NonUnitalNonAssocSemiring` and `Module R` on $A, B$; before, only `Add`, `Mul`, `SMul R` and `Star` were needed. `StarAlgEquiv.toAlgEquiv` is now the structure projection, replacing the former def, which required `[CommSemiring R] [Algebra R A]`. The new lemma `toAlgEquiv_eq_coe` states `e.toAlgEquiv = ↑e`. `coe_toAlgEquiv` is no longer `@[simp]`.

**Definition.** `symm` and `trans` now take `map_smul'` from the `AlgEquiv` versions.

**Check.** The typeclass assumptions on `StarAlgEquiv` and its API get stronger: `[Monoid R]`/`[DistribMulAction R A]` in the `Bijective` section, and `[SMul S R]` in the `Group` section, become `Semiring`/`Module`. Users with only a monoid action of $R$ lose the structure. Also, `toAlgEquiv_eq_coe` is not `@[simp]` while the neighbouring `toStarRingEquiv_eq_coe` is.
