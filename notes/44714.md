Adds that finite products and binary products of flat modules are flat, with the corresponding iff statements. Builds on `Module.Flat.directSum_iff` (for finite index, $\prod = \bigoplus$) and the rTensor-injectivity criterion `iff_rTensor_injectiveₛ` together with $P \otimes (M\times N) \cong (P\otimes M)\times(P\otimes N)$.

## pi_iff

For a commutative semiring $R$, a finite index type $\iota$ and $R$-modules $M_i$, the product $\prod_i M_i$ is flat iff every $M_i$ is flat.

**Proof.** For finite $\iota$, $\prod_i M_i \cong \bigoplus_i M_i$ (`linearEquivFunOnFintype`); flatness transfers along linear equivalences, then apply `directSum_iff`.

## pi

Instance: a finite product of flat $R$-modules is flat.

**Proof.** `pi_iff`.

## prod_iff

For a commutative semiring $R$ and $R$-modules $M, N$, $M \times N$ is flat iff $M$ and $N$ are both flat.

**Proof.** Via the criterion that $F$ is flat iff $Q \otimes F \to P \otimes F$ is injective for all submodule inclusions $Q \subseteq P$: under $P \otimes (M\times N) \cong (P\otimes M)\times(P\otimes N)$ (`prodRight`) the map becomes the product of the two maps, and a product map is injective iff both components are.

## prod

Instance: the product of two flat $R$-modules is flat.

**Proof.** `prod_iff`.
