Generalises `NormedSpace.normalize` (the unit vector $x/\|x\|$, with $0 \mapsto 0$) from real normed spaces to normed spaces over any `RCLike` field $\mathbb{K}$: now `normalize 𝕜 x` $= (\|x\|^{-1} : \mathbb{K}) \cdot x$, with the scalar field passed explicitly. The basic API follows. `normalize_smul_of_pos`/`_of_neg` now take a scalar $r \in \mathbb{K}$ with $0 < r$ or $r < 0$ in the `ComplexOrder` partial order (positive or negative real). `normalize_smul` (real scalar) is renamed `normalize_real_smul`. All Euclidean-geometry uses (angles, triangle inequality, `signedDist`) now write `normalize ℝ`.

## *

Mechanical: the declaration now writes `normalize ℝ …` where it used to write `normalize …`, or its proof passes `ℝ` to the normalize lemmas (`norm_normalize ℝ h` instead of `norm_normalize_eq_one_iff.mpr h`). Some proofs also gain `RCLike.ofReal_real_eq_id, id_eq` to remove the coercion $\mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ that now appears in `(‖x‖⁻¹ : ℝ)`. Statements are mathematically the same. What to check: only that `ℝ` is the field meant.

## normalize

For a normed space $V$ over $\mathbb{K} \in \{\mathbb{R}, \mathbb{C}\}$ and $x \in V$, $\operatorname{normalize}_{\mathbb{K}}(x) = \|x\|^{-1} \cdot x$, with $\|x\|^{-1}$ viewed as an element of $\mathbb{K}$ (so $0$ for $x = 0$).

**Definition.** As stated. `𝕜` is now an explicit argument. The `noncomputable` marker is dropped.

**Check.** The docstring still writes `normalize x`, without the field argument.

## normalize_zero_eq_zero

$\operatorname{normalize}_{\mathbb{K}}(0) = 0$.

**Proof.** `simp`, unfolding the definition.

## normalize_eq_zero_iff

$\operatorname{normalize}_{\mathbb{K}}(x) = 0 \iff x = 0$.

**Proof.** Case on $x = 0$, then `simp`.

## norm_smul_normalize

$(\|x\| : \mathbb{K}) \cdot \operatorname{normalize}_{\mathbb{K}}(x) = x$. The norm is now cast into $\mathbb{K}$ (before, the real norm acted on a real space).

**Proof.** Case on $x = 0$, then `simp`.

## norm_normalize_eq_one_iff

$\|\operatorname{normalize}_{\mathbb{K}}(x)\| = 1 \iff x \neq 0$. Its alias `norm_normalize` (the reverse direction) now takes `𝕜` explicitly.

**Proof.** Case on $x = 0$, then `simp` with `norm_smul`.

## normalize_eq_self_of_norm_eq_one

If $\|x\| = 1$ then $\operatorname{normalize}_{\mathbb{K}}(x) = x$.

**Proof.** `simp`.

## normalize_normalize

Normalizing twice is the same as normalizing once. `𝕜` is implicit here.

**Proof.** Case on $x = 0$; otherwise the normalized vector has norm 1, so `normalize_eq_self_of_norm_eq_one` applies.

## normalize_neg

$\operatorname{normalize}_{\mathbb{K}}(-x) = -\operatorname{normalize}_{\mathbb{K}}(x)$.

**Proof.** `simp`.

## normalize_smul_of_pos

For $r \in \mathbb{K}$ with $0 < r$ in the `ComplexOrder` order (so $r$ is a positive real), $\operatorname{normalize}_{\mathbb{K}}(r x) = \operatorname{normalize}_{\mathbb{K}}(x)$. This replaces the version with $r \in \mathbb{R}$, $0 < r$.

**Proof.** `simp` with `norm_smul` and `RCLike.norm_of_nonneg'` (so $\|r\| = r$).

## normalize_smul_of_neg

For $r \in \mathbb{K}$ with $r < 0$ (a negative real), $\operatorname{normalize}_{\mathbb{K}}(r x) = -\operatorname{normalize}_{\mathbb{K}}(x)$.

**Proof.** Apply the positive case to $-r$ and $-x$.

## normalize_real_smul

For $r \in \mathbb{R}$: $\operatorname{normalize}_{\mathbb{K}}((r:\mathbb{K}) x) = \operatorname{sign}(r) \cdot \operatorname{normalize}_{\mathbb{K}}(x)$. This is the old `normalize_smul` renamed, now over $\mathbb{K}$ with $r$ cast into it. `normalize_smul` stays as a deprecated alias (2026-01-04).

**Proof.** Trichotomy on $r$, then the positive and negative lemmas.

**Check.** The deprecated alias is not a drop-in replacement for the old real lemma. At $\mathbb{K} = \mathbb{R}$ the statement contains the cast `((r : ℝ) : ℝ)` (`RCLike.ofReal`), which does not match a bare `r • x` syntactically. The PR had to rewrite the proof of `signedDist_smul` for this reason. Downstream `rw [normalize_smul]` calls will break despite the deprecation.

## normalize_smul

Removed as a theorem. It is now a deprecated alias of `normalize_real_smul`.
