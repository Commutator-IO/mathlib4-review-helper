Housekeeping in `Mathlib/GroupTheory/DivisibleHull.lean` (the divisible hull $M \otimes \mathbb Q_{\ge 0}$-style construction of an ordered cancellative monoid $M$): removes every `set_option backward.privateInPublic`, the module-system escape hatch that let public declarations mention private ones. No statement changes. To make this work, the `LE` instance wraps the private `lift_aux` as `(by exact lift_aux)`, `archimedeanClassOrderIso` is marked `@[no_expose]` (its body, built from private order homs, is no longer exposed to importers), and its two `rfl` simp lemmas become `(rfl)`.

## *

Mechanical: the `set_option backward.privateInPublic true in` (and `.warn false`) lines before private helpers and public declarations are deleted. Check that nothing public still unfolds to a private definition: the only places that did are handled by `(by exact lift_aux)` in the `LE` instance, `@[no_expose]` on `archimedeanClassOrderIso`, and `(rfl)` in its two simp lemmas.

## instance

Statement unchanged: the order `LE (DivisibleHull M)`, $m/s \le n/t \iff t \cdot m \le s \cdot n$, well defined by `lift_aux`.

**Definition.** The well-definedness proof is now `(by exact lift_aux)` instead of the bare private term, so the private lemma is used inside a tactic block rather than in the exposed term.

## archimedeanClassOrderIso_apply

Statement unchanged: `archimedeanClassOrderIso M a` is the image of the Archimedean class $a$ under the map induced by the coercion $M \to \text{DivisibleHull } M$.

**Proof.** `rfl` becomes `(rfl)`: with `archimedeanClassOrderIso` now `@[no_expose]`, the definitional unfolding is only valid inside this module, and the parentheses stop Lean treating it as an `rfl`-lemma checked in the public scope.

**Check.** As a non-`rfl` lemma it is no longer used by `dsimp`, only by `simp`; probably harmless.

## archimedeanClassOrderIso_symm_apply

Statement unchanged: the inverse sends the Archimedean class of $m/s$ in the divisible hull to the class of $m$ in $M$.

**Proof.** `rfl` becomes `(rfl)` for the same reason as above.
