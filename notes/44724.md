Over a commutative Noetherian ring $A$ with an ideal $I$, shows that the $I$-primary (i.e. $I$-power torsion) component $\Gamma_I(Q) = \bigcup_n Q[I^n]$ of an injective module $Q$ is injective (Stacks 08XW (2)). The key step is a reduction of Baer's criterion for an $I$-power torsion module to extension problems along submodules of the quotients $A/I^n$, via Artin–Rees. Builds on `Ideal.primaryComponent` and `Module.Baer`.

## exists_pow_smul_eq_zero_of_primaryComponent_eq_top

(Private.) If $A$ is Noetherian, $\Gamma_I(Q) = Q$ and $g : J \to Q$ is $A$-linear on an ideal $J$, then some $I^n$ kills the whole image of $g$.

**Proof.** $J$ is finitely generated; each generator's image is killed by some $I^{e_i}$; take $n = \max e_i$ and conclude by span induction.

## baer_of_primaryComponent_eq_top

Let $A$ be a commutative Noetherian ring, $I$ an ideal and $Q$ an $A$-module with $\Gamma_I(Q) = Q$. Suppose that for every $n$, every submodule $N \subseteq A/I^n$ and every $A$-linear $f : N \to Q$, $f$ extends to an $A$-linear map $A/I^n \to Q$. Then $Q$ satisfies Baer's criterion.

**Proof.** Given $g : J \to Q$, find $n$ with $I^n$ killing $g(J)$ (helper lemma), so $g$ vanishes on $I^nJ$; Artin–Rees gives $k$ with $J \cap I^{n+k} \subseteq I^nJ$, so $g$ factors through the image of $J$ in $A/I^{n+k}$; extend this using the hypothesis and precompose with $A \to A/I^{n+k}$.

**Check.** Listed as a main result in the module docstring but has no docstring of its own (the companion `injective_of_primaryComponent_eq_top` has one).

## injective_of_primaryComponent_eq_top

Same hypotheses as `baer_of_primaryComponent_eq_top`; then $Q$ is an injective $A$-module.

**Proof.** Baer's criterion (previous theorem) implies injectivity.

## primaryComponent.injective

Instance: over a commutative Noetherian ring $A$ (with $A$ $v$-small, $Q : \mathrm{Type}\ v$), if $Q$ is an injective $A$-module then so is $\Gamma_I(Q)$.

**Proof.** Apply `injective_of_primaryComponent_eq_top` to $\Gamma_I(Q)$: it equals its own $I$-primary component; and a map $N \to \Gamma_I(Q)$ with $N \subseteq A/I^n$ extends to $A/I^n \to Q$ by injectivity of $Q$, whose image is killed by $I^n$ and so lands in $\Gamma_I(Q)$.
