Moves Heron's formula from the Archive (`Theorems100.heron`, Freek list no. 57) into `Mathlib/Geometry/Euclidean/Volume/Triangle.lean` (added in #41220), and connects it to `Simplex.volume`: the area of a triangle with sides $a, b, c$ and semiperimeter $s$ is $\sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}$. The angle form is generalised to allow degenerate triangles (no distinctness hypotheses), and `docs/100.yaml` now points to `Affine.Triangle.volume_eq_sqrt_mul_sub_mul_sub_mul_sub`.

## _root_.EuclideanGeometry.mul_sin_eq_sqrt_mul_sub_mul_sub_mul_sub

For any three points $p_1, p_2, p_3$ (possibly coinciding), with $a = |p_1p_2|$, $b = |p_1p_3|$, $c = |p_2p_3|$ and $s = (a+b+c)/2$:
$$\tfrac12\, a\, c \sin \angle p_1 p_2 p_3 = \sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}.$$
(The angle at $p_2$ is between sides $a$ and $c$, opposite $b$.)

**Proof.** The degenerate cases $p_1 = p_2$ or $p_2 = p_3$ are handled by `simp`. Otherwise the old Archive proof, relabelled: law of cosines for $\cos\gamma$, write $1 - \cos^2\gamma$ as $\bigl((2ac)^2 - (a^2+c^2-b^2)^2\bigr)/(2ac)^2$, show the numerator is nonnegative, take square roots, and factor $16\,s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)$.

**Check.** In the `calc`, the second line switches to the expression $(2ab)^2 - (a^2+b^2-c^2)^2$ (the old labelling) while the previous one uses $(2ac)^2 - (a^2+c^2-b^2)^2$; this is correct only because both equal $16s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)$ and `ring_nf` sees through it, but it reads confusingly. The theorem sits in namespace `EuclideanGeometry` via `_root_` inside the `Affine.Triangle` file.

## volume_eq_sqrt_mul_sub_mul_sub_mul_sub

**Heron's formula.** For a triangle $t$ and pairwise distinct $i_1, i_2, i_3 \in$ `Fin 3`, with $a = |P_{i_1}P_{i_2}|$, $b = |P_{i_1}P_{i_3}|$, $c = |P_{i_2}P_{i_3}|$ and $s = (a+b+c)/2$:
$$\operatorname{area}(t) = \sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}.$$

**Proof.** Rewrite the area as $\tfrac12 a c \sin \angle P_{i_1}P_{i_2}P_{i_3}$ (`volume_eq_mul_sin`) and apply the previous theorem.

**Check.** The module docstring line for `volume_eq_mul_sin` has a stray character: `$S = \frac{1}{2}ab \sin C$3`.

## volume_sq_eq_mul_sub_mul_sub_mul_sub

Same setting: $\operatorname{area}(t)^2 = s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)$ (the square-root-free form).

**Proof.** Rewrite with Heron's formula and `sq_sqrt`; nonnegativity of the radicand is shown by contradiction: if it were negative the square root, hence the area, would be $0$, contradicting `volume_pos`.
