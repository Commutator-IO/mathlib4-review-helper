States Beck's (co)monadicity theorem as a single iff: for an adjunction $F \dashv G$, the comparison functor into (co)algebras is an equivalence exactly when the relevant adjoint reflects isomorphisms and the (co)equalizers of split pairs exist and are preserved. It packages the existing one-directional constructions (`monadicOfHasPreservesGSplitCoequalizersOfReflectsIsomorphisms`, `createsGSplitCoequalizersOfMonadic` and their comonadic duals) and adds the instance that a (co)monadic adjoint reflects isomorphisms. Also adds Beck's thesis to `references.bib`.

## reflectsIsomorphisms_of_comonadicLeftAdjoint

If $F : C \to D$ is a comonadic left adjoint, then $F$ reflects isomorphisms.

**Proof.** $F$ factors as the comparison functor $C \to \mathrm{Coalg}(GF\text{-comonad})$ followed by the forgetful functor; the forgetful functor reflects isomorphisms and the comparison is fully faithful (an equivalence), so it reflects them too.

## isEquivalence_comparison_iff

(Comonadic version.) For an adjunction $F \dashv G$ with $F : C \to D$, the comparison functor $C \to \mathrm{Coalg}$ is an equivalence if and only if $F$ reflects isomorphisms and, for every pair $u, v : X \to Y$ in $C$ that is $F$-cosplit (i.e. $Fu, Fv$ have a split equalizer), the equalizer of $u, v$ exists in $C$ and is preserved by $F$.

**Proof.** ($\Rightarrow$) Build `ComonadicLeftAdjoint F` from the equivalence; reflection of isos is the instance above, and `createsFSplitEqualizersOfComonadic` gives that $F$ creates these equalizers, hence they exist (the diagram $F\circ(u,v)$ has a limit, via `diagramIsoParallelPair`) and are preserved. ($\Leftarrow$) Package the hypotheses as `HasEqualizerOfIsCosplitPair` / `PreservesLimitOfIsCosplitPair` and apply `comonadicOfHasPreservesFSplitEqualizersOfReflectsIsomorphisms`.

## reflectsIsomorphisms_of_monadicRightAdjoint

If $G : D \to C$ is a monadic right adjoint, then $G$ reflects isomorphisms.

**Proof.** Dual of the comonadic case: $G$ = forgetful functor ∘ comparison, the forgetful functor reflects isos and the comparison is fully faithful.

## isEquivalence_comparison_iff

(Monadic version.) For an adjunction $F \dashv G$ with $G : D \to C$, the comparison functor $D \to \mathrm{Alg}$ is an equivalence if and only if $G$ reflects isomorphisms and, for every $G$-split pair $u, v : X \to Y$ in $D$, the coequalizer of $u, v$ exists in $D$ and is preserved by $G$.

**Proof.** Same structure as the comonadic version: forward direction via `MonadicRightAdjoint G` and `createsGSplitCoequalizersOfMonadic`; backward direction via `monadicOfHasPreservesGSplitCoequalizersOfReflectsIsomorphisms`.
