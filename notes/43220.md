Refactors the product formula for the partition generating function `Nat.Partition.genFun` (for a character $f(i, c)$ over a commutative semiring $R$, $G_f = \sum_n \big(\sum_{p \vdash n} \prod_{i \in p} f(i, \#_p i)\big) X^n$) so that it no longer needs $R$ to be Hausdorff. The inner infinite sums $\sum_{j \ge 1} f(i, j) X^{ij}$ are replaced by an explicit power series `genFunFactor f i`, defined coefficientwise; `hasProd_genFun` now states $\prod_{i \ge 1} (1 + \text{genFunFactor } f\, i) = G_f$ for any topology on $R$, and the old tsum form survives as `hasProd_genFun'` under `[T2Space R]`. The Glaisher file switches to the primed lemma.

## toFinsuppAntidiag

Definition unchanged: sends a partition $p$ of $n$ to the finitely supported function $m \mapsto m \cdot (\text{multiplicity of } m \text{ in } p)$.

**Definition.** Only change: `@[simps]` is added, generating `toFinsuppAntidiag_apply` (and a support lemma), which lets later proofs drop `toFinsuppAntidiag` from their `simp` sets.

## coeff_genFun

Statement unchanged: the $n$-th coefficient of $G_f$ is $\sum_{p \vdash n} \prod_{i} f(i, \#_p i)$. Listed only because the docstring of the following lemma changed.

**Proof.** Unchanged (`PowerSeries.coeff_mk`).

## summable_genFun_term

Removed from this position (it needed the order-tends-to-infinity argument). It reappears below, after `hasSum_genFunFactor`, with the same statement and a new proof.

## summable_genFun_term'

Removed from this position; reappears below `hasSum_genFunFactor` with the same statement.

## genFunFactor

For $f : \mathbb N \to \mathbb N \to R$ and $i \in \mathbb N$, the power series
$$\text{genFunFactor } f\, i = \sum_{n \ne 0,\ i \mid n} f(i, n/i)\, X^n = \sum_{j \ge 1} f(i, j) X^{ij}.$$
For $i = 0$ this is $0$.

**Definition.** `PowerSeries.mk` with coefficient `if n ≠ 0 ∧ i ∣ n then f i (n / i) else 0`; no topology involved.

## constantCoeff_genFunFactor

The constant coefficient of `genFunFactor f i` is $0$ (simp lemma).

**Proof.** `simp` on the definition.

## coeff_genFunFactor

For $i \ne 0$ and $j \ne 0$, the coefficient of $X^{j i}$ in `genFunFactor f i` is $f(i, j)$ (simp lemma).

**Proof.** `simp` on the definition.

## coeff_genFunFactor_self

For $i \ne 0$, the coefficient of $X^i$ in `genFunFactor f i` is $f(i, 1)$ (simp lemma).

**Proof.** `coeff_genFunFactor` with $j = 1$.

## aux_dvd_of_coeff_ne_zero

Old signature (with hypothesis $0 \notin s$ and the tsum factor) removed here; the new version follows below.

## dvd_of_coeff_genFunFactor_ne_zero

If the $n$-th coefficient of `genFunFactor f i` is nonzero, then $i \mid n$.

**Proof.** `simp_all` on the definition.

## aux_prod_f_eq_prod_coeff

(Private.) For a partition $p$ of $n$ and a finset $s \supseteq \{1, \dots, n\}$: $\prod_{i \in p} f(i, \#_p i) = \prod_{i \in s} [X^{i \cdot \#_p i}]\,(1 + \text{genFunFactor } f\, i)$. Compared with the old version the hypothesis $0 \notin s$ is gone and the factor is `genFunFactor`.

**Proof.** `prod_subset_one_on_sdiff`: off the parts of $p$ the coefficient is that of $X^0$, i.e. $1$; on a part $i$ it is $f(i, \#_p i)$ by `coeff_genFunFactor`. Much shorter than before (no `tsum_eq_single`).

## aux_dvd_of_coeff_ne_zero

(Private.) If $g \in s.\text{finsuppAntidiag}\ d$ and every coefficient $[X^{g(i)}](1 + \text{genFunFactor } f\, i)$, $i \in s$, is nonzero, then $x \mid g(x)$ for every $x$. The hypothesis $0 \notin s$ is dropped.

**Proof.** If $x \in s$ and $g(x) \ne 0$, the coefficient of $1$ vanishes and `dvd_of_coeff_genFunFactor_ne_zero` applies; if $x \notin s$ then $g(x) = 0$.

## aux_prod_coeff_eq_zero_of_notMem_range

Statement unchanged up to the factor: (private) for $0 \notin s$ and $g \in s.\text{finsuppAntidiag}\ d$ not of the form `toFinsuppAntidiag p`, $\prod_{i \in s} [X^{g(i)}](1 + \text{genFunFactor } f\, i) = 0$.

**Proof.** Same argument as before (build the partition with $g(i)/i$ copies of $i$); only tidied and adapted to the new `aux_dvd_of_coeff_ne_zero`.

## aux_prod_f_eq_prod_coeff

## hasSum_genFunFactor

For any topology on $R$ (coefficientwise topology on $R⟦X⟧$) and $i \ne 0$: $\sum_{j \ge 0} f(i, j+1)\, X^{i(j+1)}$ has sum `genFunFactor f i`. This is what links the new factor to the old infinite sums.

**Proof.** The map $j \mapsto i(j+1)$ is injective and the coefficients outside its range vanish, so `Function.Injective.hasSum_iff` reduces to `PowerSeries.hasSum_of_monomials_self`.

## summable_genFun_term

Statement unchanged: $j \mapsto f(i+1, j+1)\, X^{(i+1)(j+1)}$ is summable (any topology on $R$).

**Proof.** Now `(hasSum_genFunFactor f _).summable`. The docstring was dropped.

## summable_genFun_term'

Statement unchanged: for $i \ne 0$, $j \mapsto f(i, j+1)\, X^{i(j+1)}$ is summable.

**Proof.** `(hasSum_genFunFactor f hi).summable`. The docstring was dropped.

## hasProd_genFun

New statement: for any topology on $R$, $\prod_{i \ge 0} (1 + \text{genFunFactor } f\,(i+1))$ converges (unconditionally) to $G_f$, i.e. $G_f = \prod_{i \ge 1}(1 + \sum_{j \ge 1} f(i, j) X^{ij})$ without needing $T_2$. The old statement (factors written as `tsum`) is now `hasProd_genFun'`.

**Proof.** Same as before: coefficientwise, the finite partial products stabilise; the $d$-th coefficient of the product is a sum over `finsuppAntidiag`, matched with partitions of $d$ via `toFinsuppAntidiag` (`sum_of_injOn`) using the two private lemmas.

**Check.** Breaking change of statement under the same name (no deprecation possible); downstream uses of the tsum form must move to `hasProd_genFun'`.

## hasProd_genFun

(Second hunk of the same proof.) The final step calls `aux_prod_f_eq_prod_coeff` without the dropped $0 \notin s$ argument.

## multipliable_genFun

## multipliable_one_add_genFunFactor

For any topology on $R$, $i \mapsto 1 + \text{genFunFactor } f\,(i+1)$ is multipliable.

**Proof.** `(hasProd_genFun f).multipliable`.

**Check.** The deprecated alias `multipliable_genFun` points to a lemma with a different statement (factor `genFunFactor` instead of the tsum), so it is not a drop-in replacement.

## hasProd_genFun'

For $R$ with a $T_2$ topology: $\prod_{i \ge 0} \big(1 + \sum'_{j} f(i+1, j+1)\, X^{(i+1)(j+1)}\big)$ has product $G_f$ — the old statement of `hasProd_genFun`.

**Proof.** `convert hasProd_genFun f`, rewriting each factor with `(hasSum_genFunFactor f _).tsum_eq` (uniqueness of limits needs $T_2$).

## genFun_eq_tprod

**Proof.** `(hasProd_genFun f).tprod_eq.symm`.

**Check.** The statement changed from the tsum form under the same name, and no version with the old form is kept (unlike `hasProd_genFun'`).
