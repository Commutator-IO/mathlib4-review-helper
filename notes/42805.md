Builds the continuous functional calculus for self-adjoint operators on a Hilbert space over $\mathbb{K} = \mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, including real Hilbert spaces. It reuses the C⋆-algebra CFC of the complexified operator $T_{\mathbb{C}}$ on $E_{\mathbb{C}}$ from #42744. For real $g$, $g(T_{\mathbb{C}})$ commutes with conjugation and with multiplication by $i$, so it decomplexifies to an operator on $E$. With this CFC available, `StarOrder.lean` can make `StarOrderedRing (H →L[𝕜] H)` an instance for every `RCLike 𝕜`, not only $\mathbb{C}$. The PR also proves that complexification commutes with `cfc`, `cfcₙ`, `CFC.sqrt` and `CFC.abs`. Its copy of `Complexification/Basic.lean` is an older revision of #42744: it lacks `realLinearIsometryEquivComplex_apply`/`_symm_apply` and `TensorProduct.lean`, so a rebase is needed.

## IsSelfAdjoint.norm_add_eq_max

(Protected, in `ContinuousLinearMap`.) For self-adjoint operators $S, T$ on a $\mathbb{K}$-Hilbert space with $ST = 0$: $\|S + T\| = \max(\|S\|, \|T\|)$.

**Proof.** Complexify, which is isometric. Apply the C⋆-algebra lemma `IsSelfAdjoint.norm_add_eq_max` to $S_{\mathbb{C}}$ and $T_{\mathbb{C}}$, using $S_{\mathbb{C}}T_{\mathbb{C}} = (ST)_{\mathbb{C}} = 0$.

**Check.** Its full name is `ContinuousLinearMap.IsSelfAdjoint.norm_add_eq_max`, so `hS.norm_add_eq_max` dot notation resolves to the root C⋆-algebra lemma, not this one. That lemma does not apply when $\mathbb{K} = \mathbb{R}$. A different name may be better.

## conjugateStarAlgEquiv

**Definition.** The map $T \mapsto \overline{T}$, $\overline{T}(v) = \overline{T(\overline{v})}$ (`Complexification.conjugate`), as an $\mathbb{R}$-linear star-algebra automorphism of $B(E_{\mathbb{C}})$. It is conjugate-linear over $\mathbb{C}$.

## symm_conjugateStarAlgEquiv

`conjugateStarAlgEquiv` is its own inverse.

**Proof.** `rfl`.

## conjugateStarAlgEquiv_comp_cfcHom_toComplexification

For self-adjoint $T$ on $E$, composing $\mathrm{cfcHom}(T_{\mathbb{C}}) : C(\sigma(T_{\mathbb{C}}), \mathbb{R}) \to B(E_{\mathbb{C}})$ with conjugation gives back $\mathrm{cfcHom}(T_{\mathbb{C}})$.

**Proof.** Uniqueness of the CFC (`cfcHom_eq_of_continuous_of_map_id`): the composite is continuous and sends $\mathrm{id}$ to $\overline{T_{\mathbb{C}}} = T_{\mathbb{C}}$.

## conjugate_cfcHom_toComplexification

Consequently, for every real continuous $g$ on $\sigma(T_{\mathbb{C}})$: $\overline{g(T_{\mathbb{C}})} = g(T_{\mathbb{C}})$.

**Proof.** Rewrite with the previous lemma.

## commute_cfcHom_toComplexification_algebraMapCLM_I

(private) $g(T_{\mathbb{C}})$ commutes with the complexification of multiplication by $i \in \mathbb{K}$. When $\mathbb{K} = \mathbb{R}$ this $i$ is $0$.

**Proof.** $T_{\mathbb{C}}$ commutes with that operator, so $g(T_{\mathbb{C}})$ does too (`commute_cfcHom`).

## cfcHomAux

(private) **Definition.** For self-adjoint $T$, the $\mathbb{R}$-star-algebra hom $C(\sigma_{\mathbb{R}}(T_{\mathbb{C}}), \mathbb{R}) \to B(E)$ sending $g$ to the decomplexification of $g(T_{\mathbb{C}})$ (`ofComplexification`). The decomplexification exists by the two lemmas above. Assumes `[NormedSpace ℝ E] [IsScalarTower ℝ 𝕜 E]`. Multiplicativity and the star are checked through the injective map `toComplexification`.

## toComplexification_cfcHomAux

(private) $(\mathrm{cfcHomAux}_T\, g)_{\mathbb{C}} = g(T_{\mathbb{C}})$.

**Proof.** `toComplexification_ofComplexification`, using the commutation and conjugation lemmas.

## instance

For every operator $a$ on $E$, $\sigma_{\mathbb{R}}(a)$ is compact.

**Proof.** $\sigma_{\mathbb{R}}(a) = \sigma_{\mathbb{R}}(a_{\mathbb{C}})$ (`spectrum_toComplexification_real`), and the latter is compact in the C⋆-algebra $B(E_{\mathbb{C}})$.

## instCFC

For a $\mathbb{K}$-Hilbert space $E$ (with compatible real structure), $B(E)$ has a continuous functional calculus over $\mathbb{R}$ for self-adjoint operators.

**Proof.** The CFC hom is `cfcHomAux`. Each axiom transfers through the isometric, injective map $T \mapsto T_{\mathbb{C}}$ and the equality of real spectra:
- continuity;
- injectivity, from injectivity of `cfcHom`;
- $\mathrm{id} \mapsto T$;
- the spectral mapping property;
- self-adjointness of the image.

Spectra are nonempty because $B(E_{\mathbb{C}})$ is nontrivial.

## spectralRadius_toComplexification

For self-adjoint $T$: the spectral radius of $T_{\mathbb{C}}$ over $\mathbb{C}$ equals the spectral radius of $T$ over $\mathbb{K}$.

**Proof.** For self-adjoint operators both equal $\|T\|_+$, and complexification preserves the norm.

## instIsometricCFC

The real CFC on $B(E)$ is isometric: $\|g(T)\| = \sup_{\sigma(T)}|g|$.

**Proof.** $g(T)$ is self-adjoint, so its norm is its spectral radius. That equals the real spectral radius of $g(T)_{\mathbb{C}}$, which by spectral mapping is $\sup |g|$ over $\sigma(T)$.

## toComplexificationStarAlgHom

**Definition.** $T \mapsto T_{\mathbb{C}}$ as an $\mathbb{R}$-star-algebra hom $B(E) \to B(E_{\mathbb{C}})$.

## continuous_toComplexificationStarAlgHom

That hom is continuous (`@[fun_prop]`).

**Proof.** `continuous_toComplexification`.

## toComplexification_cfc

For every $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ and every operator $T$ on $E$: $(f(T))_{\mathbb{C}} = f(T_{\mathbb{C}})$. The junk cases agree because self-adjointness and the spectrum are preserved.

**Proof.** Case split on self-adjointness and on continuity of $f$ on the spectrum. The main case is `StarAlgHom.map_cfc` for `toComplexificationStarAlgHom`; the other cases are junk values $0 = 0$.

## toComplexification_cfcₙ

The same for the non-unital calculus: $(\mathrm{cfc}_n f\,T)_{\mathbb{C}} = \mathrm{cfc}_n f\,T_{\mathbb{C}}$.

**Proof.** Case split on $f(0) = 0$, self-adjointness and continuity. In the main case, reduce to `cfc` via `cfcₙ_eq_cfc`.

## toComplexification_sqrt

For every operator $T$ on $H$: $(\sqrt{T})_{\mathbb{C}} = \sqrt{T_{\mathbb{C}}}$, where `CFC.sqrt` is $0$ off the nonnegative operators.

**Proof.** If $T \ge 0$, use `CFC.sqrt_eq_real_sqrt` on both sides and `toComplexification_cfc`. Otherwise both sides are $0$ (nonnegativity is preserved by complexification).

## toComplexification_abs

$(|T|)_{\mathbb{C}} = |T_{\mathbb{C}}|$.

**Proof.** Unfold `CFC.abs` $= \sqrt{T^*T}$ and use `simp`.
