Adds the generating function `genFunDistincts f` $= \sum_n \big(\sum_{p \vdash n \text{ distinct}} \prod_{i \in p} f(i)\big) X^n$ for partitions into distinct parts, with the product formula $\prod_{i \ge 1}(1 + f(i) X^i)$, as a special case of `Nat.Partition.genFun`. Taking $f \equiv -1$ and comparing with Euler's pentagonal number theorem (`hasProd_one_sub_X_pow`, already in mathlib) gives the classical combinatorial corollary: the number of partitions of $n$ into an even number of distinct parts minus those into an odd number is the coefficient of the pentagonal series, so the two counts agree iff $n$ is not a generalised pentagonal number. The `genFunFactor` refactor comes from #43220, but this PR sits on an older version of it (no `@[simps]` on `toFinsuppAntidiag`, and `summable_genFun_term`/`summable_genFun_term'` deleted rather than kept); it should be rebased.

## oddCountDistincts

**Definition.** For $n \in \mathbb N$, the finset of partitions of $n$ into distinct parts with an odd number of parts.

## evenCountDistincts

**Definition.** For $n \in \mathbb N$, the finset of partitions of $n$ into distinct parts with an even number of parts.

## genFunDistincts

For a commutative semiring $R$ and $f : \mathbb N \to R$, the power series $\sum_n \big(\sum_{p} \prod_{i \in p} f(i)\big) X^n$, the inner sum over partitions $p$ of $n$ into distinct parts.

**Definition.** `PowerSeries.mk` of $\sum_{p \in \text{distincts } n} \prod (p.\text{parts}.\text{map } f)$.

**Check.** Unlike `genFun` it is not marked `noncomputable`; fine if it compiles, but inconsistent with its neighbour.

## coeff_genFunDistincts

The $n$-th coefficient of `genFunDistincts f` is $\sum_{p \vdash n \text{ distinct}} \prod_{i \in p} f(i)$ (simp lemma).

**Proof.** `PowerSeries.coeff_mk`.

## genFunDistincts_eq_genFun

`genFunDistincts f = genFun g` where $g(i, c) = f(i)$ if $c = 1$ and $0$ otherwise (`Pi.single 1 (f i) c`).

**Proof.** Coefficientwise, `sum_of_injOn id`: a partition with a repeated part $i$ ($\#i > 1$) contributes a zero factor $g(i, \#i)$; for a partition with distinct parts all counts are $1$ and the `Finsupp.prod` becomes the multiset product.

## hasProd_genFunDistincts

For any topology on $R$: $\prod_{i \ge 0} (1 + f(i+1) X^{i+1})$ has product `genFunDistincts f`, i.e. $\sum_n (\dots) X^n = \prod_{i \ge 1}(1 + f(i) X^i)$.

**Proof.** Rewrite with `genFunDistincts_eq_genFun` and apply `hasProd_genFun`; for this character `genFunFactor g (i+1)` is just $f(i+1) X^{i+1}$, checked coefficientwise.

## genFunDistincts_eq_tprod

For $R$ with a $T_2$ topology: `genFunDistincts f` $= \prod'_{i} (1 + f(i+1) X^{i+1})$.

**Proof.** `(hasProd_genFunDistincts f).tprod_eq.symm`.

## hasProd_powerSeriesMk_card_evenCountDistincts_sub_card_oddCountDistincts

For a commutative ring $R$ with any topology: $\prod_{i \ge 1} (1 - X^i) = \sum_n \big(\#\text{evenCountDistincts}(n) - \#\text{oddCountDistincts}(n)\big) X^n$ (difference taken in $R$).

**Proof.** `hasProd_genFunDistincts` with $f \equiv -1$; the coefficient $\sum_p (-1)^{\#\text{parts}}$ is split by the parity of the number of parts (`sum_filter`, `sum_sub_distrib`).

**Check.** Declared without `public` in a `module` file that has no `public section`, so this theorem is private to the file while the two below are `public`; probably it should be public too.

## card_evenCountDistincts_sub_card_oddCountDistincts

For any commutative ring $R$ and $n \in \mathbb N$: $\#\text{evenCountDistincts}(n) - \#\text{oddCountDistincts}(n) = [X^n]\,\text{pentagonalSeries } R$ in $R$, i.e. $(-1)^k$ if $n$ is the $k$-th generalised pentagonal number $k(3k-1)/2$, $k \in \mathbb Z$, and $0$ otherwise.

**Proof.** Put the discrete topology on $R$; both the previous lemma and the pentagonal number theorem `hasProd_one_sub_X_pow` give a product for $\prod (1 - X^{i+1})$, so the limits agree (`HasProd.unique`); compare $n$-th coefficients.

**Check.** Docstring typo "coefficent"; double space before `:=`.

## card_evenCountDistincts_eq_card_oddCountDistincts_iff

For $n \in \mathbb N$: the number of partitions of $n$ into an even number of distinct parts equals the number into an odd number of distinct parts iff $n$ is not a generalised pentagonal number ($n \notin \{k(3k-1)/2 : k \in \mathbb Z\}$).

**Proof.** Take $R = \mathbb Z$ in the previous lemma and use `coeff_pentagonalSeries_eq_zero_iff`, `sub_eq_zero` and injectivity of the cast.
