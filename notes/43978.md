Proves the generating function of the Bessel functions of integer order (DLMF 10.12.1): for every $x \in \mathbb{C}$ and $t \ne 0$, $\sum_{a\in\mathbb{Z}} J_a(x)\,t^a = \exp\!\big(\tfrac{x}{2}(t - t^{-1})\big)$, with summability and analyticity of the series in $x$. Along the way it adds exponential bounds on $J_a$ and on the regularized ${}_0F_q$ function, and Gamma-function comparisons. It builds on the derivative recurrence from #42825 and the `@[fun_prop]` tags from #45102 (whose versions of #42825's proofs here are slightly older than #42825's head).

## AnalyticAt.differentiableWithinAt

Statement unchanged: a function analytic at $x$ is differentiable at $x$ within any set. Only change: tagged `@[fun_prop]`, as in #45102 (whose `## *` note is not borrowed by the site, hence this section).

## norm_besselJ_le_exp

For $a \in \mathbb{C}$ with $\operatorname{Re} a \ge 0$ and any $x \in \mathbb{C}$:
$$|J_a(x)| \le \frac{|(x/2)^a|}{|\Gamma(a+1)|}\, e^{|x/2|^2}.$$
(For $a$ a pole of $\Gamma(a+1)$ the inverse is $0$ in Lean, but $\operatorname{Re} a \ge 0$ excludes that.)

**Proof.** Unfold $J_a$ and apply `regularizedHGFun_le_exp_of_one_le_re` to $b = \{a+1\}$, $z = -(x/2)^2$.

## norm_besselJ_le_exp_int

For $a \in \mathbb{Z}$ and $x \in \mathbb{C}$: $|J_a(x)| \le \dfrac{|x/2|^{|a|}}{|a|!}\, e^{|x/2|^2}$.

**Proof.** Reduce to $a \ge 0$ via $J_{-a}(x) = J_a(-x)$, then the previous lemma with $\Gamma(a+1) = a!$.

## besselJ_mul_pow_le

(Private.) For fixed $t$ and a bounded set $s \subseteq \mathbb{C}$ there are constants $u, v$ with $|J_a(x)\,t^a| \le \frac{u^{|a|}\,|t^a|}{|a|!}\, v$ for all $a \in \mathbb{Z}$, $x \in s$.

**Proof.** Take $u = |x_0/2|$, $v = e^{|x_0/2|^2}$ for a bound $|x| \le |x_0|$ on $s$, and use `norm_besselJ_le_exp_int`.

## summable_bound

(Private.) For real $u, v$ and $t \in \mathbb{C}$, $\sum_{a\in\mathbb{Z}} \frac{u^{|a|}|t^a|}{|a|!}\,v$ is summable.

**Proof.** Split into $a \ge 0$ and $a < 0$; each half is a multiple of the exponential series at $u|t|$, resp. $u|t|^{-1}$.

## analyticOnNhd_tsum_besselJ_mul_pow

For each $t \in \mathbb{C}$, $x \mapsto \sum_{a\in\mathbb{Z}} J_a(x)\,t^a$ is analytic on all of $\mathbb{C}$.

**Proof.** On each unit ball the terms are entire and dominated by the summable bound above, so the sum is differentiable there (`differentiableOn_tsum_of_summable_norm`); complex-differentiable on an open set implies analytic.

## summable_besselJ_mul_pow

For all $x, t \in \mathbb{C}$, $a \mapsto J_a(x)\,t^a$ is summable over $\mathbb{Z}$.

**Proof.** Comparison with `summable_bound` on the set $\{x\}$.

## tsum_besselJ_mul_pow

Generating function: for $x \in \mathbb{C}$ and $t \ne 0$,
$$\sum_{a\in\mathbb{Z}} J_a(x)\,t^a = \exp\!\Big(\frac{x}{2}\,(t - t^{-1})\Big).$$

**Proof.** Both sides are entire in $x$, so by the identity theorem it suffices to prove equality on a ball around $0$ where the left side is non-zero (it equals $1$ at $x=0$). There, both have the same logarithmic derivative $(t-t^{-1})/2$: differentiate the series termwise, use $2J_a' = J_{a-1} - J_{a+1}$ (`two_mul_deriv_besselJ_int`) and reindex $a \mapsto a \pm 1$; equal log-derivatives plus equal values at $0$ give equality (`logDeriv_eqOn_iff`).

## hasSum_besselJ_mul_pow

The `HasSum` form of the previous theorem: for $t \ne 0$, $\sum_{a\in\mathbb{Z}} J_a(x)\,t^a$ converges to $\exp(\frac{x}{2}(t-t^{-1}))$.

**Proof.** Summability plus `tsum_besselJ_mul_pow`.

## norm_Gamma_le_norm_Gamma_add_one

For $s \in \mathbb{C}$ with $|s| \ge 1$: $|\Gamma(s)| \le |\Gamma(s+1)|$.

**Proof.** $\Gamma(s+1) = s\,\Gamma(s)$ ($s \ne 0$) and $|s| \ge 1$.

## norm_Gamma_le_norm_Gamma_add_of_one_le_re

For $s \in \mathbb{C}$ with $\operatorname{Re} s \ge 1$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$: $|\Gamma(s)| \le |\Gamma(s+n)|$.

**Proof.** Induction on $n$ with the previous lemma, using $|s+k| \ge \operatorname{Re}(s+k) \ge 1$.

## regularizedHGFun_le_exp_of_one_le_re

For a multiset $b$ of complex parameters, all with $\operatorname{Re} b_j \ge 1$, and $z \in \mathbb{C}$, the regularized ${}_0F_q$ function (no upper parameters) satisfies
$$|\mathbf{F}(\,;b;z)| \le \Big|\prod_j \Gamma(b_j)\Big|^{-1} e^{|z|}.$$

**Proof.** Compare the series termwise with $|\prod\Gamma(b_j)|^{-1}\sum |z|^n/n!$: each coefficient is $1/(n!\prod_j\Gamma(b_j+n))$ and $|\Gamma(b_j)| \le |\Gamma(b_j+n)|$ by `norm_Gamma_le_norm_Gamma_add_of_one_le_re`.

**Check.** The statement bounds a norm; by mathlib naming it should probably be `norm_regularizedHGFun_le_exp_of_one_le_re` (and `norm_besselJ_le_exp` above follows that convention).
