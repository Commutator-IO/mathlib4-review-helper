For a tower of commutative rings $R \to S \to T$, shows that the first fundamental sequence $0 \to T\otimes_S \Omega_{S/R} \to \Omega_{T/R} \to \Omega_{T/S} \to 0$ is split exact when $H^1(L_{T/S}) = 0$ and $\Omega_{T/S}$ is a projective $T$-module, e.g. for $T$ formally smooth over $S$ (Matsumura, Thm. 25.1). It factors out the injectivity of $T\otimes_S\Omega_{S/R} \to \Omega_{T/R}$ (from the Jacobi–Zariski exact sequence) as a separate lemma and reuses it to shorten the proof of the existing `tensorKaehlerEquivOfFormallyEtale` (statement unchanged).

## KaehlerDifferential.mapBaseChange_injective

For commutative rings $R \to S \to T$ forming a scalar tower, if $H^1(L_{T/S})$ (`Algebra.H1Cotangent S T`) is zero, then the canonical $T$-linear map $T \otimes_S \Omega_{S/R} \to \Omega_{T/R}$, $t \otimes ds \mapsto t\,ds$, is injective.

**Proof.** Exactness of $H^1(L_{T/S}) \xrightarrow{\delta} T\otimes_S\Omega_{S/R} \to \Omega_{T/R}$ (`exact_δ_mapBaseChange`): the kernel is the image of a zero module.

## KaehlerDifferential.exists_retraction_mapBaseChange

Same setting; if $H^1(L_{T/S}) = 0$ and $\Omega_{T/S}$ is a projective $T$-module, then there is a $T$-linear $l : \Omega_{T/R} \to T\otimes_S\Omega_{S/R}$ with $l \circ (T\otimes_S\Omega_{S/R} \to \Omega_{T/R}) = \mathrm{id}$, i.e. the first fundamental sequence is split exact.

**Proof.** The sequence $T\otimes_S\Omega_{S/R} \to \Omega_{T/R} \to \Omega_{T/S} \to 0$ is exact and the left map is injective (previous lemma); by `Function.Exact.split_tfae`, a retraction exists iff the surjection $\Omega_{T/R} \to \Omega_{T/S}$ has a section, which follows from projectivity of $\Omega_{T/S}$ (`Module.projective_lifting_property`).

## KaehlerDifferential.tensorKaehlerEquivOfFormallyEtale

Statement unchanged: for $R \to S \to T$ with $T$ formally étale over $S$, base change gives an isomorphism of $T$-modules $T \otimes_S \Omega_{S/R} \cong \Omega_{T/R}$.

**Proof.** Now one line: `mapBaseChange` is bijective, injective by the new `mapBaseChange_injective` (formally smooth suffices) and surjective by exactness of the fundamental sequence, since $\Omega_{T/S} = 0$.
