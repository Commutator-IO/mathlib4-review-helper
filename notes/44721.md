Adds the formula for the distance from a point to a hyperplane given by a normal vector: $\operatorname{dist}(p, \pi_s p) = |\langle n, p - q\rangle|/\|n\|$ for any $q$ in the hyperplane. It is stated for an affine subspace $s$ whose orthogonal complement of the direction is $\mathbb R n$, and specialised to the hyperplane $q + (\mathbb R n)^\perp$ built with `AffineSubspace.mk'`. It uses `EuclideanGeometry.orthogonalProjection` and renames the section `perpBisector` to `RealInnerProductSpace`. An anonymous instance supplies `HasOrthogonalProjection` for the direction of `mk' q K`.

## dist_orthogonalProjection_eq_abs_inner_div_norm

Let $V$ be a real inner product space, $P$ a Euclidean affine space over $V$, and $s$ a nonempty affine subspace whose direction admits an orthogonal projection. Suppose $(\operatorname{dir} s)^\perp = \mathbb R n$ for some $n \in V$. Then for every $q \in s$ and $p \in P$, $\operatorname{dist}(p, \pi_s(p)) = |\langle n, p - q\rangle|/\|n\|$. (If $n = 0$ both sides are $0$.)

**Proof.** $p - \pi_s(p) \in (\operatorname{dir} s)^\perp$, so $p - \pi_s(p) = t n$. Moreover $\langle n, \pi_s(p) - q\rangle = 0$ since $\pi_s(p) - q \in \operatorname{dir} s$. Hence $\langle n, p - q\rangle = t\|n\|^2$ while $\operatorname{dist} = |t|\,\|n\|$. Conclude by `field`.

## dist_orthogonalProjection_mk'_eq_abs_inner_div_norm

For $q, p \in P$ and $n \in V$, the distance from $p$ to its orthogonal projection on the affine hyperplane $q + (\mathbb R n)^\perp$ is $|\langle n, p - q\rangle|/\|n\|$.

**Proof.** Apply the previous theorem, with $(\mathbb R n)^{\perp\perp} = \mathbb R n$ (`Submodule.orthogonal_orthogonal`) and $q \in \text{mk}'\,q\,K$.
