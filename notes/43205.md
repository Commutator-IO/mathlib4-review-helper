Proves that a field extension is separable when its degree is smaller than the (positive) characteristic. The key input is a characteristic-free version of "an irreducible polynomial whose degree is nonzero in $F$ is separable", which needs the degree formula for the derivative under the weaker hypothesis that multiplication by $\deg p$ is injective on $R$ (`IsSMulRegular`) instead of torsion-freeness. The existing torsion-free lemmas `mem_support_derivative` and `degree_derivative` become corollaries, and the `CharZero` results in `Separable.lean` are moved into their own section.

## mem_support_derivative_of_isSMulRegular

For a semiring $R$, $p \in R[X]$ and $n \in \mathbb N$ such that $x \mapsto (n+1) \cdot x$ is injective on $R$: the coefficient of $X^n$ in $p'$ is nonzero iff the coefficient of $X^{n+1}$ in $p$ is nonzero.

**Proof.** The coefficient of $X^n$ in $p'$ is $(n+1)\, p_{n+1}$; regularity gives $(n+1)\, a = 0 \Rightarrow a = 0$ (`grind` with `IsSMulRegular.right_eq_zero_of_smul`).

## mem_support_derivative

Old proof removed here (it used `smul_eq_zero` under torsion-freeness); the lemma is restated below as a corollary of `mem_support_derivative_of_isSMulRegular`.

## degree_derivative

Old version removed here; its proof becomes that of `degree_derivative_of_isSMulRegular`, and the lemma is restated below.

## degree_derivative_of_isSMulRegular

For a nontrivial semiring $R$ and $p \in R[X]$ such that multiplication by $\deg p$ (as a natural number) is injective on $R$: $\deg p' = \operatorname{natDegree} p - 1$ (as a `WithBot ℕ` degree, with truncated subtraction).

**Proof.** $\le$ as before (bound each term of the derivative). For $\ge$: regularity forces $\operatorname{natDegree} p \ge 1$ (multiplication by $0$ is not injective on a nontrivial ring), and then the coefficient of $X^{\deg p - 1}$ in $p'$ is nonzero by `mem_support_derivative_of_isSMulRegular` and the leading coefficient being nonzero.

## natDegree_derivative_of_isSMulRegular

For a semiring $R$ and $p$ with multiplication by $\operatorname{natDegree} p$ injective on $R$: $\operatorname{natDegree} p' = \operatorname{natDegree} p - 1$ (truncated subtraction).

**Proof.** Trivial if $R$ is trivial; otherwise from `degree_derivative_of_isSMulRegular`.

## mem_support_derivative

Statement unchanged: under the torsion-free hypothesis (`HasUniqueDiv R`), $X^n$ is in the support of $p'$ iff $X^{n+1}$ is in the support of $p$.

**Proof.** Now one line: `mem_support_derivative_of_isSMulRegular` with `IsSMulRegular.of_ne_zero`.

## degree_derivative

Statement unchanged: under the torsion-free hypothesis, if $\operatorname{natDegree} p \ne 0$ then $\deg p' = \operatorname{natDegree} p - 1$.

**Proof.** The trivial ring contradicts the hypothesis; otherwise `degree_derivative_of_isSMulRegular` with `IsSMulRegular.of_ne_zero`.

## _root_.Irreducible.separable_of_natCast_natDegree_ne_zero

For a field $F$ and an irreducible $f \in F[X]$ whose degree is nonzero in $F$ (i.e. the characteristic does not divide $\deg f$): $f$ is separable.

**Proof.** In a field, $\deg f \ne 0$ in $F$ makes multiplication by $\deg f$ injective, so $\deg f' = \deg f - 1 \ne -\infty$, i.e. $f' \ne 0$; conclude with `separable_iff_derivative_ne_zero`.

**Check.** The existing `Irreducible.separable [CharZero F]` just above is now a special case and could be derived from this lemma.

## IsSeparable.of_integral_of_natCast_natDegree_ne_zero

For a field $F$, a domain $K$ that is an integral $F$-algebra, and $x \in K$ whose minimal polynomial has degree nonzero in $F$: $x$ is separable over $F$.

**Proof.** The minimal polynomial is irreducible; apply the previous lemma.

## Algebra.IsSeparable.of_integral_of_finrank_lt

For a field $F$ and a domain $K$ that is a finite (and integral) $F$-algebra with $[K : F] < \operatorname{char} F$ (`ringChar`): $K/F$ is separable. Vacuous in characteristic $0$.

**Proof.** For each $x$, $0 < \deg \mu_x \le [K:F] < p$, so $p \nmid \deg \mu_x$ (`Nat.not_dvd_of_pos_of_lt`), hence $\deg \mu_x \ne 0$ in $F$ (`ringChar.spec`); apply the previous lemma.

**Check.** The section variable `[Algebra.IsIntegral F K]` is implied by `[Module.Finite F K]` (instance `Algebra.IsIntegral.of_finite`), so it is a redundant hypothesis here; consider stating the lemma outside that section and dropping `of_integral` from the name.

## Algebra.IsSeparable.of_integral_of_rank_lt

Same with the cardinal rank: if $\operatorname{rank}_F K < \operatorname{char} F$ (as cardinals) then $K/F$ is separable (domain $K$, integral over $F$).

**Proof.** The rank is below $\aleph_0$, so $K$ is finite over $F$; then `of_integral_of_finrank_lt`, comparing via `Cardinal.toNat`.

**Check.** As above, `[Algebra.IsIntegral F K]` is redundant since the hypothesis already forces finite dimension.
