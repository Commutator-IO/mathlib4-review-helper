Generalises `ContinuousAlgEquiv.eq_continuousLinearEquivConjContinuousAlgEquiv` from seminormed spaces to topological vector spaces with separating dual: every continuous algebra isomorphism $\mathcal{L}(V) \cong \mathcal{L}(W)$ is conjugation by a continuous linear isomorphism $V \cong W$. Its proof used the normed, fully continuous `apply'` and `smulRightL`. These are replaced by TVS versions that are continuous only in the later arguments: `smulRightₗ'` from #43113, and the new `ContinuousLinearMap.applyₗ'` / `applyₗ` (evaluation at a vector). Also adds `rfl` lemmas linking the normed `apply'` / `apply` to the new maps, and updates their docstrings.

## toLinearMap_apply'

Simp lemma: in the normed setting (with `RingHomIsometric σ₁₂`), the continuous evaluation map `apply' F σ₁₂ : E →SL (E →SL F) →L F`, viewed as a semilinear map in $v$, equals `applyₗ' F σ₁₂`.

**Proof.** `rfl`.

## toLinearMap_apply

Simp lemma: the linear version, `(apply 𝕜 Fₗ).toLinearMap = applyₗ 𝕜 Fₗ`.

**Proof.** `rfl`.

## ContinuousAlgEquiv.eq_continuousLinearEquivConjContinuousAlgEquiv

For a nontrivially normed field $\mathbb{k}$ and topological vector spaces $V, W$ over $\mathbb{k}$ (topological additive groups, continuous scalar multiplication) whose continuous duals separate points: every continuous $\mathbb{k}$-algebra isomorphism $f : \mathcal{L}(V) \to \mathcal{L}(W)$ is $A \mapsto U A U^{-1}$ for some continuous linear isomorphism $U : V \to W$. Previously $V, W$ had to be seminormed spaces.

**Proof.** Unchanged in strategy: pick $u \ne 0$, $v \in V'$ with $v(u) \ne 0$, $z$ with $f(v \otimes u)z \ne 0$; $T x = f(v \otimes x) z$ is a bijective continuous intertwiner, with continuous inverse $T' w = f^{-1}(d \otimes w) u$. $T$ and $T'$ are now built from `applyₗ` and `smulRightₗ'` instead of the normed `apply'` / `smulRightL`. The final `continuous_invFun` step now unfolds `LinearEquiv.ofBijective` by `ext; simp`. The `set_option backward.isDefEq.respectTransparency.types false` is removed.

## _root_.ContinuousLinearMap.applyₗ'

For normed fields $\mathbb{k}, \mathbb{k}_2$, $\sigma : \mathbb{k} \to \mathbb{k}_2$, a topological vector space $E$ (continuous scalar multiplication) and $F$ a topological additive group with continuous constant scalar multiplication: the $\sigma$-semilinear map $E \to \mathcal{L}(\mathcal{L}_\sigma(E, F), F)$, $x \mapsto (f \mapsto f(x))$. Evaluation at $x$ is continuous for the topology of bounded convergence.

**Definition.** Built by hand: the inner map is $f \mapsto f x$, with additivity and homogeneity by `rfl` and continuity by the auto-param. The outer laws by `ext; simp`. `@[simps apply_apply]`.

**Check.** It is declared as `_root_.ContinuousLinearMap.applyₗ'` inside `namespace ContinuousLinearEquiv`, `section Semilinear`, whose `variable`s include `[RingHomIsometric σ₁₂]`. Since $\sigma_{12}$ appears in the type, that instance is probably picked up as an unused argument. Confirm with `#check @ContinuousLinearMap.applyₗ'`. A `ContinuousLinearMap` section would be a more natural home.

## _root_.ContinuousLinearMap.applyₗ

The linear case: for $E$ a topological vector space and $F$ as above over a normed field $\mathbb{k}$, the $\mathbb{k}$-linear map $E \to \mathcal{L}(\mathcal{L}(E, F), F)$, $x \mapsto (f \mapsto f x)$.

**Definition.** `applyₗ' F (RingHom.id 𝕜)`, with `@[simps! apply_apply]`.

**Check.** It is also placed under `namespace ContinuousLinearEquiv` (section `Linear`) via `_root_`. Same placement remark as for `applyₗ'`.
