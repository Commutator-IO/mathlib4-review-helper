Introduces the "corner simplex" in $\mathbb{R}^n$ (`EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin n)`): vertex $0$ at the origin and vertex $i \ge 1$ at the standard unit vector $e_{i-1}$. It records that its faces through the origin are again corner simplices (of lower dimension, embedded isometrically by extending by zero), and that every altitude from a non-origin vertex has foot $0$ and length $1$. It also defines, for any $n$-simplex $s$ in a Euclidean affine space, the affine map $\mathbb{R}^n \to P$ sending the corner simplex onto $s$, and computes the `normDet` (absolute determinant w.r.t. orthonormal bases) of its linear part as $|\text{volume form of the edge vectors}|$. Intended as a tool for volume computations.

## cornerSimplex

**Definition.** The $n$-simplex in `EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin n)` with $P_0 = 0$ and $P_i = e_{i-1}$ for $i = 1, \dots, n$. Affine independence: the vectors $P_i - P_0$ are exactly the standard basis vectors, which are linearly independent. Has `@[simps]` (so `cornerSimplex_points` is a simp lemma).

**Check.** Docstring: "All but one of the altitudes of this simplex has unit length" (grammar: "have").

## cornerSimplexFacePoints

**Definition.** For an order embedding $f : \{0..m-1\} \hookrightarrow \{0..n-1\}$, the index set $\{0\} \cup \{f(j) + 1 : j < m\} \subseteq$ `Fin (n+1)`: the vertices of the corner simplex lying in the coordinate subspace spanned by the image of $f$, used as the index set for `Simplex.face`.

**Check.** Docstring typo "deminsional".

## card_cornerSimplexFacePoints

The index set above has $m + 1$ elements. Simp lemma.

**Proof.** `simp` (0 is not a successor; the maps are injective).

## cornerSimplexFacePoints_succAboveOrderEmb

For $i \in$ `Fin (n+1)` and $f = $ `i.succAbove` (the embedding $\{0..n-1\} \to \{0..n\}$ skipping $i$), the index set is the complement $\{i+1\}^c$ in `Fin (n+2)`. Simp lemma.

**Proof.** Extensionality, case split on whether the index is $0$ or a successor, `simp`.

## orderEmbOfFin_cornerSimplexFacePoints

The increasing enumeration of `cornerSimplexFacePoints f` is $0, f(0)+1, \dots, f(m-1)+1$ (i.e. `Fin.cons 0 (fun j ↦ (f j).succ)`). Simp lemma.

**Proof.** Uniqueness of the increasing enumeration (`orderEmbOfFin_unique`): this list lies in the set and is strictly monotone.

## face_cornerSimplex

For an order embedding $f : $ `Fin m ↪o Fin n`, the face of the $n$-dimensional corner simplex on the index set `cornerSimplexFacePoints f` equals the image of the $m$-dimensional corner simplex under the linear isometry $\mathbb{R}^m \to \mathbb{R}^n$ that places coordinate $j$ at coordinate $f(j)$ and fills the rest with zeros (`piLpExtendByZero`).

**Proof.** Vertex by vertex, case split on $0$ / successor, `simp`.

## faceOpposite_cornerSimplex

The face of the $(n+1)$-dimensional corner simplex opposite vertex $i+1$ (i.e. opposite $e_i$) is the $n$-dimensional corner simplex embedded by extension by zero along `i.succAbove` (skipping coordinate $i$).

**Proof.** Special case of `face_cornerSimplex` via `cornerSimplexFacePoints_succAboveOrderEmb`.

## altitudeFoot_cornerSimplex

For $n \ne 0$ and $i \ne 0$, the foot of the altitude from vertex $i$ of the corner simplex is the origin. Simp lemma.

**Proof.** The origin lies in the opposite face's span, and $e_{i-1}$ is orthogonal to its direction, spanned by the other $e_j$ (`inner_single_left`, closed by `aesop`).

## height_cornerSimplex

For $n \ne 0$ and $i \ne 0$, the height from vertex $i$ of the corner simplex is $1$. Simp lemma.

**Proof.** `simp` with the definition of height (distance from $e_{i-1}$ to the foot $0$).

## cornerMap

**Definition.** For an $n$-simplex $s$ in $P$, the affine map $\mathbb{R}^n \to P$, $x \mapsto \sum_i x_i (P_{i+1} - P_0) + P_0$, i.e. the unique affine map sending the corner simplex's vertices to those of $s$.

**Check.** The docstring calls it the map "from `cornerSimplex` to an arbitrary simplex"; its domain is the whole `EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin n)`. Minor.

## cornerMap_zero

`s.cornerMap 0` $= P_0$. Simp lemma.

**Proof.** `simp`.

## cornerMap_single

`s.cornerMap` $e_i = P_{i+1}$. Simp lemma.

**Proof.** `simp`.

## cornerMap_injective

`s.cornerMap` is injective.

**Proof.** Reduce to injectivity of the linear part, which is `Basis.constr` on the linearly independent family $P_{i+1} - P_0$ (from affine independence of $s$).

## map_cornerSimplex_cornerMap

The image of the corner simplex under `s.cornerMap` is $s$. Simp lemma.

**Proof.** Vertex-wise, using `cornerMap_zero` and `cornerMap_single`.

## normDet_cornerMap_linear

If $V$ has dimension $n$ and $o$ is an orientation of $V$, the norm-determinant of the linear part of `s.cornerMap` equals
$$\bigl|\omega_o(P_1 - P_0, \dots, P_n - P_0)\bigr|,$$
where $\omega_o$ is the volume form of $o$.

**Proof.** Rewrite the edge vectors as images of the standard basis vectors, compute `normDet` via the matrix in the standard basis of $\mathbb{R}^n$ and an orthonormal basis of $V$ (`normDet_eq_norm_det_toMatrix`), and identify that determinant with the volume form up to sign (`volumeForm_robust'`).
