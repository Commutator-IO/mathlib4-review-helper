Adds elementary comparisons of $|\Gamma(s)|$ with $|\Gamma(s+1)|$ and $|\Gamma(s+n)|$ for complex $s$, from the functional equation $\Gamma(s+1) = s\,\Gamma(s)$. Used to bound regularized hypergeometric coefficients (and Bessel functions, #43978).

## norm_Gamma_le_norm_Gamma_iff

For $s \in \mathbb{C}$: $|\Gamma(s)| \le |\Gamma(s+1)|$ iff $s = 0$ or $|s| \ge 1$. (Mathlib's $\Gamma$ is $0$ at the poles $0,-1,-2,\dots$, where the left side is $0$.)

**Proof.** If $\Gamma(s) = 0$ then $s = -m$ and both sides hold (`grind`). Otherwise $s \ne 0$, $\Gamma(s+1) = s\Gamma(s)$, and divide by $|\Gamma(s)| > 0$.

**Check.** The name does not say which two Gamma values are compared; `norm_Gamma_le_norm_Gamma_add_one_iff` would match the next lemma. #43978 adds the next two lemmas again (in `Gamma/Basic.lean`, with a different proof): the PRs will clash on names.

## norm_Gamma_le_norm_Gamma_add_one

For $s \in \mathbb{C}$ with $|s| \ge 1$: $|\Gamma(s)| \le |\Gamma(s+1)|$.

**Proof.** Right-to-left direction of the previous iff.

## norm_Gamma_le_norm_Gamma_add_of_one_le_re

For $s \in \mathbb{C}$ with $\operatorname{Re} s \ge 1$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$: $|\Gamma(s)| \le |\Gamma(s+n)|$. The docstring says the bound is not tight and is for convenience.

**Proof.** Induction on $n$, applying the previous lemma at $s+k$, where $|s+k| \ge \operatorname{Re}(s+k) \ge 1$.
