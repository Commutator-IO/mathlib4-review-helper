New file `Analysis/Matrix/StarAlgEquiv.lean`. It proves a Skolem–Noether statement for full matrix algebras: every $K$-algebra automorphism of $M_n(K)$ is conjugation by an invertible matrix. Its star version says every $\star$-algebra automorphism of $M_n(\mathbb{K})$ ($\mathbb{K} = \mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$) is conjugation by a unitary matrix. The bridge is a general lemma: when the range of a star-preserving map has trivial centralizer, conjugating by a unit and conjugating by a unitary give the same family of maps. The PR builds on `Unitary.conjStarAlgAut` and `MulSemiringAction.toAlgEquiv` for `ConjAct`. Two `proof_wanted` statements cover isomorphisms $M_m \cong M_n$.

## StarHom.coe_eq_units_conjugate_iff_coe_eq_unitary_conjugate

Let $\mathbb{K}$ be $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ and $A$ a star-ordered $\mathbb{K}$-algebra (star module, partial order with `StarOrderedRing`). Let $B$ be a type with a star and $f, g : B \to A$ star-preserving maps (only `StarHomClass` is assumed; neither map has to be multiplicative or linear). Assume the centralizer of $f(B)$ in $A$ is $\mathbb{K}\cdot 1$. Then the following are equivalent:
- $g = y\,f(\cdot)\,y^{-1}$ for some unit $y \in A^\times$;
- $g = u\,f(\cdot)\,u^*$ for some unitary $u$.

**Proof.** Unitary ⇒ unit is immediate. For the converse, apply $\star$ to $g(x^*) = g(x)^*$: this shows that $y^*y$ commutes with every $f(x)$, so $y^*y = \alpha\cdot 1$. Then $\alpha$ is real (self-adjointness), nonzero ($y^*y$ is a unit) and positive ($0 \le y^*y$, using the order). So $u = \alpha^{-1/2}y$ is unitary and gives the same conjugation.

**Check.** The docstring says "⋆-homomorphisms", but the statement only needs star-preserving maps. The `StarHom.` prefix is also odd for a lemma about any `StarHomClass`. The proof is long (about 50 lines, built from a chain of `this`/`replace this`) and could be split up.

## AlgEquiv.eq_mulSemiringActionToAlgEquiv_conjAct

For a field $K$ and finite $n$, every $K$-algebra automorphism $f$ of $M_n(K)$ is inner: there is $U \in GL_n(K)$ with $f(x) = UxU^{-1}$ for all $x$. Here $f$ equals the automorphism induced by $U$ acting through `ConjAct`.

**Proof.** Transport $f$ along `Matrix.toLinAlgEquiv'` to an automorphism of $\operatorname{End}_K(K^n)$. Apply `AlgEquiv.eq_linearEquivConjAlgEquiv`, which gives conjugation by a linear equivalence, and turn that back into an invertible matrix. The rest is a `simp` computation.

**Check.** The statement is specific to matrices but lives in the `AlgEquiv` namespace. A `Matrix.`-prefixed name (or the planned generalisation to central simple algebras) would fit better.

## StarAlgEquiv.eq_unitaryConjStarAlgAut

For $\mathbb{K} = \mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, every $\star$-algebra automorphism $f$ of $M_n(\mathbb{K})$ is conjugation by a unitary: $f = \operatorname{Ad}(U)$, that is $f(x) = UxU^*$, for some $U$ in the unitary group of $n\times n$ matrices.

**Proof.** The previous theorem gives an invertible $g$ with $f = \operatorname{Ad}(g)$. Then apply `StarHom.coe_eq_units_conjugate_iff_coe_eq_unitary_conjugate` with the identity as the first map. Its range is all of $M_n(\mathbb{K})$, whose centralizer is the scalars (by `simp`, via `Algebra.IsCentral`). The result is a unitary $U$ with the same conjugation.
