Proves **Feuerbach's theorem** (Freek list no. 29): the nine-point circle of a triangle is internally tangent to the incircle and externally tangent to each of the three excircles. Following Scheer's vector proof, it computes $|I_S N|^2 = (r_S \pm \rho)^2$ for the in/excenter $I_S$, the nine-point center $N$ and nine-point radius $\rho = R/2$, using Euler-type distance formula `dist_excenter_sq` from #44230 together with new formulas for the distance from a vertex to the nine-point center (via a general vertex-to-Euler-line distance formula for $n$-simplices). Registers the result in `docs/100.yaml` and `docs/1000.yaml`, adds the reference to `references.bib`.

## inner_vsub_circumcenter_vsub_circumcenter

For an $n$-simplex with circumcenter $O$ and circumradius $R$, and vertex indices $i, j$:
$$\langle P_i - O,\; P_j - O\rangle = R^2 - \tfrac12 |P_iP_j|^2.$$

**Proof.** Polarisation identity, with $|P_iO| = |P_jO| = R$; `ring`.

## dist_lineMap_circumcenter_centroid_sq

For an $n$-simplex with circumcenter $O$, centroid $G$, circumradius $R$, a vertex $P_i$ and $x \in \mathbb{R}$, let $X = O + x(G - O)$ (a point on the Euler line) and $t = x/(n+1)$. Then
$$|P_i X|^2 = (1-x)^2 R^2 + (1-t)\,t \sum_{j \ne i} |P_iP_j|^2 - \frac{t^2}{2} \sum_{j \ne i}\sum_{k \ne i} |P_jP_k|^2.$$

**Proof.** Long `calc`: write $P_i - X = (1-x)(P_i - O) + x(P_i - G)$, expand $P_i - G = \frac1{n+1}\sum_{j\ne i}(P_i - P_j)$ (`vsub_centroid_eq`), re-centre everything at $O$, expand the squared norm and substitute `inner_vsub_circumcenter_vsub_circumcenter`; finish with `field`.

## dist_excenter_ninePointCircle_center_sq

(Private.) For a triangle and signs $S \in \{\emptyset, \{0\}, \{1\}, \{2\}\}$, with in/excenter $I_S$, radius $r_S$, nine-point center $N$ and nine-point radius $\rho$:
$$|I_S N|^2 = (r_S + \varepsilon \rho)^2, \qquad \varepsilon = -1 \text{ if } S = \emptyset, \text{ else } +1.$$

**Proof.** A `calc`: apply `dist_excenter_sq` at $p = N$, substitute the vertex-to-$N$ distances $(R^2 + b^2 + c^2 - a^2)/4$, case-split on the four sign sets to factor the side-length expression as Heron's product, replace it by $16S^2$ (`volume_sq_eq_mul_sub_mul_sub_mul_sub`), then use $4SR = abc$ and $S = \frac12 r_S\sum w$ and $\rho = R/2$.

**Check.** Long computational proof with a four-way case split under `all_goals` with `simp` + `ring`; possibly fragile.

## isExtTangent_exsphere_ninePointCircle

**Feuerbach's theorem, excircles.** For a triangle and any vertex index $i$, the excircle with signs $\{i\}$ (the excircle opposite vertex $i$) and the nine-point circle are externally tangent.

**Proof.** By `isExtTangent_iff_dist_center` it suffices that $|I_iN| = r_i + \rho$ with both radii nonnegative; square both sides and use the private lemma.

## isIntTangent_insphere_ninePointCircle

**Feuerbach's theorem, incircle.** For a triangle, the incircle and the nine-point circle are internally tangent.

**Proof.** $V$ is nontrivial (two distinct edge vectors); by `isIntTangent_iff_dist_center` it suffices that $|IN| = |r - \rho|$; since $r \le \rho$ (`inradius_le_ninePointCircle_radius`) this is $\rho - r$, and the squared form is the private lemma.

## ninePointCircle_center_eq_lineMap_circumcenter_centroid

For an $n$-simplex, the nine-point center is $O + \frac{n+1}{n}(G - O)$, i.e. `lineMap O G ((n+1)/n)`.

**Proof.** Unfold `ninePointCircle_center` and `lineMap_apply`.

## ninePointCircle_center_eq_point

For a 0-simplex (a single point $P_0$), the nine-point center is $P_0$.

**Proof.** `simp` with the previous lemma and the circumcenter of a point being that point.

## ninePointCircle_radius_nonneg

The nine-point radius of any simplex is nonnegative.

**Proof.** It is $R/n$ with $R \ge 0$.

## dist_ninePointCircle_center_sq

For an $n$-simplex with nine-point center $N$ and circumradius $R$, and a vertex $P_i$:
$$|P_iN|^2 = \frac{R^2 + (n-1)\sum_{j\ne i}|P_iP_j|^2 - \frac12\sum_{j\ne i}\sum_{k\ne i}|P_jP_k|^2}{n^2}.$$
(For $n = 0$ both sides are $0$, the right by Lean's division by zero.)

**Proof.** Case $n = 0$ by `ninePointCircle_center_eq_point`; otherwise `dist_lineMap_circumcenter_centroid_sq` at $x = (n+1)/n$ and `field`.

## dist_ninePointCircle_center_sq

Triangle version (namespace `Affine.Triangle`): for pairwise distinct $i_1, i_2, i_3$, with $a = |P_{i_2}P_{i_3}|$ the opposite side,
$$|P_{i_1}N|^2 = \frac{R^2 + |P_{i_1}P_{i_2}|^2 + |P_{i_1}P_{i_3}|^2 - |P_{i_2}P_{i_3}|^2}{4}.$$

**Proof.** The simplex version with $n = 2$, $\{i_1\}^c = \{i_2, i_3\}$, expanding the double sum over the pair; `ring`.

## vsub_centroid_eq

For a simplex over a characteristic-zero field and any point $p$: $p - G = \frac{1}{n+1}\sum_x (p - P_x)$.

**Proof.** Negate the existing `centroid_vsub_eq`.
