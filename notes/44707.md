Adds an openness criterion in Noetherian quasi-sober spaces (e.g. underlying spaces of Noetherian schemes): a set stable under generalization is open as soon as, for each of its points $x$, it contains a nonempty open subset of $\overline{\{x\}}$. This is the topological input of standard "constructible + stable under generalization ⇒ open"-type arguments. Imports `Mathlib.Topology.Sober` for generic points.

## NoetherianSpace.isOpen_of_stableUnderGeneralization

Let $X$ be a Noetherian, quasi-sober space and $E \subseteq X$ stable under generalization. Suppose that for each $x \in E$ there is an open $W \ni x$ with $W \cap \overline{\{x\}} \subseteq E$. Then $E$ is open.

**Proof.** Noetherian (well-founded) induction on closed sets $Z$: if $Z \setminus E$ is dense in $Z$, then $Z \cap E = \emptyset$. If $Z$ is irreducible with generic point $\xi$ and $y \in Z \cap E$, then $\xi \in E$ by generalization-stability; the open $W \ni \xi$ from the hypothesis meets $Z \setminus E$ by density, contradicting $W \cap \overline{\{\xi\}} \subseteq E$. If $Z = (Z\cap z_1) \cup (Z \cap z_2)$ is reducible, apply the induction hypothesis to the strictly smaller closed sets $\overline{Z \cap z_i \setminus E}$. Finally apply the claim to $Z = \overline{E^c}$ to get $\overline{E^c} \subseteq E^c$.
