Shows that every module is $I$-adically complete when the ideal $I$ is nilpotent, since the $I$-adic filtration becomes zero after finitely many steps. The existing (anonymous) instance that an Artinian local ring is complete for its maximal ideal is reproved as a one-line consequence, using that its maximal ideal is nilpotent.

## of_isNilpotent

For a commutative ring $R$, an ideal $I$ with $I^k = 0$ for some $k$, and any $R$-module $M$, $M$ is $I$-adically complete (`IsAdicComplete I M`: Hausdorff and precomplete).

**Proof.** For $n \ge k$, $I^n M = 0$. Hausdorff: $x \equiv 0 \bmod I^k M = 0$ forces $x = 0$. Precomplete: for a Cauchy sequence $(f_n)$ the limit is $f_k$; for $n \le k$ use the Cauchy condition directly, for $n \ge k$ the congruence modulo $I^k M = 0$ is equality.
