Relates incenters/excenters of an $n$-simplex to volumes. Mathlib defines the (unnormalised) excenter weights as $\pm 1/h_i$ (inverse heights, sign $-$ for indices in `signs`); since $\operatorname{vol}(s) = \frac1n h_i \operatorname{vol}(F_i)$ with $F_i$ the face opposite vertex $i$, these are proportional to the signed face volumes $\pm\operatorname{vol}(F_i)$. The PR defines these signed face volumes, transfers the basic API to them, and derives $\operatorname{vol}(s) = \frac1n r \cdot \bigl|\sum_i \pm\operatorname{vol}(F_i)\bigr|$ for an exradius $r$, in particular $\operatorname{vol}(s) = \frac1n\, r_{\text{in}} \cdot (\text{surface area})$. New file `Geometry/Euclidean/Volume/Incenter.lean`.

## excenterWeightsFace

**Definition.** For an $n$-simplex $s$ ($n \ne 0$), a set of indices `signs` and a vertex index $i$, the real number
$$w_i = \begin{cases} -\operatorname{vol}(F_i) & i \in \text{signs} \\ \operatorname{vol}(F_i) & i \notin \text{signs}\end{cases}$$
where $F_i$ is the $(n-1)$-face opposite vertex $i$. An unnormalised weight vector for the excenter with the given signs, alternative to `excenterWeightsUnnorm`.

## excenterWeightsFace_ne_zero

$w_i \ne 0$ for every $i$ and every choice of signs.

**Proof.** `positivity` after unfolding (face volume is positive, the sign is $\pm1$).

## excenterWeightsFace_empty

With no signs (the incenter case), $w_i = \operatorname{vol}(F_i)$. Simp lemma.

**Proof.** `simp` on the definition.

## excenterWeightsFace_eq_volume_mul_excenterWeightsUnnorm

$w_i = n \cdot \operatorname{vol}(s) \cdot u_i$, where $u_i = \pm h_i^{-1}$ is `excenterWeightsUnnorm signs i`.

**Proof.** Rewrite $\operatorname{vol}(s) = \frac1n h_i \operatorname{vol}(F_i)$ (`volume_eq`) and unfold both weights; `field` using $n \ne 0$.

## excenterWeightsFace_reindex

For a bijection $e$ of vertex indices (`Fin (n+1) ≃ Fin (m+1)`), the face weights of the reindexed simplex with signs $S$ are those of $s$ with signs $e^{-1}(S)$, precomposed with $e^{-1}$.

**Proof.** Derive $n = m$, then reduce to `excenterWeightsUnnorm_reindex` via the previous lemma.

## excenterWeightsFace_map

Mapping the simplex by an affine isometric embedding $f$ does not change the face weights. Simp lemma.

**Proof.** Extensionality and `simp` (face volumes are invariant).

## excenterWeightsFace_restrict

Restricting the simplex to an affine subspace $S$ containing its span does not change the face weights. Simp lemma.

**Proof.** Extensionality and `simp` with `faceOpposite_restrict`.

## excenterExists_iff_sum_excenterWeightsFace_ne_zero

The excenter with the given signs exists (`ExcenterExists`, defined as $\sum_i u_i \ne 0$) iff $\sum_i w_i \ne 0$.

**Proof.** `simp` with $w_i = n \operatorname{vol}(s) u_i$, pulling the constant out of the sum, using $n \ne 0$ and $\operatorname{vol}(s) > 0$.

## excenterWeights_eq_excenterWeightsFace_div

The normalised excenter weights are $w_i / \sum_j w_j$.

**Proof.** Substitute $w_i = n \operatorname{vol}(s) u_i$, factor out of the sum, `field`. (No existence hypothesis needed: both sides use Lean's $x/0 = 0$.)

## ExcenterExists.volume_eq_exradius_mul

If the excenter with the given signs exists, then
$$\operatorname{vol}(s) = \frac1n \, r_{\text{signs}} \, \Bigl|\sum_i w_i\Bigr|,$$
with $r_{\text{signs}}$ the corresponding exradius.

**Proof.** Substitute $w_i = n\operatorname{vol}(s)u_i$, use `exradius_eq_abs_inv_sum` ($r = |\sum_i u_i|^{-1}$) and positivity of the volume, then `field` using $\sum u_i \ne 0$.

## volume_eq_inradius_mul

$$\operatorname{vol}(s) = \frac1n \, r_{\text{in}} \sum_i \operatorname{vol}(F_i),$$
the volume is $\frac1n$ times the inradius times the total surface area.

**Proof.** The previous lemma with empty signs (the incenter always exists), dropping the absolute value since the sum is positive.
