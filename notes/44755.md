Stacked on #44736, whose diff it includes; these notes cover only the declarations not already in #44736 (see the notes for #44736 for `forall_hasseDeriv_eq_zero_iff`, `forall_hasseDeriv_eq_zero_iff_exists_expand`, `hasseDeriv_eq_zero_iff`, `pow_dvd_iff_forall_dvd_choose`). It shows that over a ring of prime characteristic $p$, the Hasse derivative $D^{(k)}$ with $k < p^e$ is linear over the subring of polynomials in $X^{p^e}$; in particular it preserves the ideal $(X^{p^e})$ and so descends to $R[X]/(X^{p^e})$. The general tool is a Leibniz-rule corollary in `Polynomial/HasseDeriv.lean`.

## hasseDeriv_X_pow_mul

For a ring $R$ of prime characteristic $p$, $k < p^e$ and $g \in R[X]$: $D^{(k)}(X^{p^e} g) = X^{p^e} D^{(k)} g$.

**Proof.** Apply `hasseDeriv_mul_of_forall_hasseDeriv_eq_zero` with $f = X^{p^e}$; the needed vanishing $D^{(i)} X^{p^e} = 0$ for $0 < i \le k$ comes from `forall_hasseDeriv_eq_zero_iff`, since the only exponent of $X^{p^e}$ is $p^e$.

**Check.** No docstring although listed as a main statement in the module doc; it is the special case $f = X$ of `hasseDeriv_expand_mul` (but stated for non-commutative rings).

## hasseDeriv_expand_mul

For a commutative ring $R$ of prime characteristic $p$, $k < p^e$ and $f, g \in R[X]$: $D^{(k)}\big(f(X^{p^e})\, g\big) = f(X^{p^e})\, D^{(k)} g$.

**Proof.** `hasseDeriv_mul_of_forall_hasseDeriv_eq_zero`, with the vanishing $D^{(i)} f(X^{p^e}) = 0$ for $0 < i \le k < p^e$ given by `forall_hasseDeriv_eq_zero_iff_exists_expand`.

## hasseDeriv_mul_of_forall_hasseDeriv_eq_zero

For a semiring $R$, $k \in \mathbb{N}$ and $f, g \in R[X]$: if $D^{(i)} f = 0$ for all $0 < i \le k$, then $D^{(k)}(fg) = f\, D^{(k)} g$.

**Proof.** Leibniz rule `hasseDeriv_mul`: $D^{(k)}(fg) = \sum_{i+j=k} D^{(i)} f \, D^{(j)} g$; all terms with $i \neq 0$ vanish by hypothesis, leaving $D^{(0)} f \, D^{(k)} g = f\, D^{(k)} g$ (`Finset.sum_eq_single`).

**Check.** No docstring, although listed in the module doc.
