Adds `@[fun_prop]` to about 25 existing lemmas in `Analysis/Calculus/FDeriv/Analytic.lean` deriving differentiability from analyticity / power-series expansions (`HasFPowerSeries*.hasFDerivAt`, `AnalyticOn(Nhd).differentiableOn`, `AnalyticOnNhd.fderiv`, `AnalyticOnNhd.deriv`, `AnalyticOnNhd.iterated_deriv`, the inverse-function `OpenPartialHomeomorph.analyticAt_symm`, the `HasFiniteFPowerSeriesOnBall` variants, ...). No statement or proof changes.

## *

Statement and proof unchanged; the lemma is only tagged `@[fun_prop]`, so `fun_prop` can use it to prove differentiability (or analyticity of a derivative) from analyticity.

**Check.** `fun_prop` can only chain through a lemma if its hypothesis is itself a registered `fun_prop` property: `AnalyticAt` and `AnalyticOnNhd` are, but `HasFPowerSeriesAt`, `HasFPowerSeriesWithinAt`, `HasFPowerSeries(Within)OnBall`, `HasFiniteFPowerSeriesOnBall`, `AnalyticWithinAt` and `AnalyticOn` do not appear to be tagged in `Analytic/Basic.lean`, so those tags may be inert; likewise conclusions at `insert x s`, at `x + y`, or with a free derivative `i` (`analyticAt_symm'`, `analyticAt_symm`) rarely match a goal. Worth confirming each tag is usable (or that `fun_prop` does not warn). The tag on `AnalyticOnNhd.deriv` overlaps #42825 and the one on `AnalyticAt.differentiableWithinAt` overlaps #43978.
