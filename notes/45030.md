Improves the continuity results for currying in the compact-open topology. `continuous_curry` now only assumes $X$ locally compact (instead of $X \times Y$), and new variants show that currying $C(X \times Y, Z) \to C(X, C(Y, Z))$ and uncurrying (with $Y$ locally compact) are continuous when $X$ is an `R1Space` (e.g. Hausdorff), giving a homeomorphism `Homeomorph.curryOfR1Space` alongside the existing `Homeomorph.curry`. Module docs and the `curry` docstring are updated accordingly.

## continuous_curry

(Statement changed.) If $X$ is locally compact, then currying $C(X \times Y, Z) \to C(X, C(Y, Z))$ is continuous for the compact-open topologies. Previously the hypothesis was that $X \times Y$ is locally compact.

**Proof.** Reduce (by `continuous_of_continuous_uncurry`) to continuity of $(f, x) \mapsto f(x, \cdot) \in C(Y,Z)$. Given a compact $L \subseteq Y$ and open $U \subseteq Z$ with $f(\{x\}\times L) \subseteq U$, the tube lemma plus local compactness of $X$ give a compact neighbourhood $C$ of $x$ with $f(C \times L) \subseteq U$; then $f' (C \times L) \subseteq U$ for $f'$ near $f$ (`eventually_mapsTo`), which is the required neighbourhood.

**Check.** Not strictly a generalization: `LocallyCompactSpace (X × Y)` does not imply `LocallyCompactSpace X` when $Y$ is empty, so any caller relying only on a product instance would break (callers with instances on both factors are fine).

## continuous_curry_of_r1Space

If $X$ is an $R_1$ space, then currying $C(X \times Y, Z) \to C(X, C(Y, Z))$ is continuous.

**Proof.** For $R_1$ $X$ the compact-open topology on $C(X, \cdot)$ is tested on restrictions to compact $K \subseteq X$ (`tendsto_compactOpen_iff_forall`); on each compact $K$ (locally compact) apply `continuous_curry` after precomposing with $K \times Y \to X \times Y$.

**Check.** No docstring (also on `continuous_uncurry_of_r1Space`).

## continuous_uncurry_of_r1Space

If $X$ is an $R_1$ space and $Y$ is locally compact, then uncurrying $C(X, C(Y, Z)) \to C(X \times Y, Z)$ is continuous.

**Proof.** Test on compact $M \subseteq X \times Y$: with $K = \pi_1(M)$ compact, the restriction to $M$ factors as restriction $C(X,\cdot) \to C(K,\cdot)$, the existing `continuous_uncurry` on $K \times Y$ (both locally compact), and precomposition with $M \to K \times Y$.

## curryOfR1Space

For $X$ an $R_1$ space and $Y$ locally compact, currying is a homeomorphism $C(X \times Y, Z) \cong C(X, C(Y, Z))$.

**Definition.** `ContinuousMap.curry` with inverse `uncurry` (inverse identities by `ext; rfl`), continuity from the two lemmas above.

## curry

Docstring only: currying a continuous map $f : X \times Y \to Z$ gives a continuous map $X \to C(Y, Z)$; the docstring now says the currying map itself is continuous when $X$ is locally compact or $R_1$ (it used to say "$X \times Y$ locally compact"), and fixes the variable names (`α × β` → `X × Y`).
