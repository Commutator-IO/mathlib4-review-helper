Proves the Cauchy–Binet formula for matrices over a commutative ring $R$: the determinant of a product $AB$ of an $n\times\iota$ and an $\iota\times n$ matrix is the sum over $n$-element subsets $s \subseteq \iota$ of the products of the corresponding maximal minors. The proof uses the basis of the exterior power $\bigwedge^n R^\iota$ built earlier in `ExteriorPower/Basis.lean` and the pairing `pairingDual` between $\bigwedge^n$ of a module and of its dual. It also gives the version for arbitrary $n\times n$ minors of $AB$.

## det_mul_eq_sum_powersetCard

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $\iota$ a finite linearly ordered type, $A$ an $n\times\iota$ matrix and $B$ an $\iota\times n$ matrix. Then
$$\det(AB) = \sum_{\substack{s\subseteq\iota\\ |s| = n}} \det\big(A_{[n],s}\big)\,\det\big(B_{s,[n]}\big),$$
where $A_{[n],s}$ keeps all rows of $A$ and the columns in $s$ (in increasing order, via `powersetCard.ofFinEmbEquiv`), and $B_{s,[n]}$ keeps the rows in $s$. (If $n > |\iota|$ the sum is empty and both sides are $0$.)

**Proof.** Expands $\iota\text{Multi}(\text{rows of }A) \in \bigwedge^n R^\iota$ in the basis of $\bigwedge^n R^\iota$ coming from the standard basis of $R^\iota$, and applies the pairing with $\iota\text{Multi}$ of the $n$ linear forms $\sum_k B_{kj}\,\mathrm{proj}_k$; both sides of the resulting equality are identified by one large `simpa ... using!`.

**Check.** The whole proof is a single `congr(...)`, `eta_expand` and a `simpa [...] using!` over many lemmas; it may be fragile.

## det_submatrix_mul

Cauchy–Binet for minors: for $A$ an $l\times\iota$ matrix, $B$ an $\iota\times o$ matrix and row/column selections $I : \{0,\dots,n-1\}\to l$, $K : \{0,\dots,n-1\}\to o$,
$$\det\big((AB)_{I,K}\big) = \sum_{\substack{s\subseteq\iota\\ |s| = n}} \det\big(A_{I,s}\big)\,\det\big(B_{s,K}\big).$$
($I$ and $K$ need not be injective or monotone.)

**Proof.** $(AB)_{I,K} = A_{I,\cdot}\,B_{\cdot,K}$ (`submatrix_mul`), then apply `det_mul_eq_sum_powersetCard`.
